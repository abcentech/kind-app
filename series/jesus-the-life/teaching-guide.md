### A note on "John 8 — I am the life"

Jesus' words "I am the life" are in **John 14:6** ("I am the way, the truth, and the life") and **John 11:25** ("I am the resurrection, and the life"). John 8 has the closely related **John 8:12**: "I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life."

All three are in the series. John 8:12 pairs beautifully with John 1:4 ("the life was the light of men"), so it carries Episode 17. John 14:6 and 11:25 carry the summit of the month.

## 1. What we are teaching

### The definition — taught on Day 1, drilled all month

> **LIFE is God's own life — found IN HIM, living IN ME, flowing THROUGH ME.**

Three fingers, like September:

- **IN HIM** — life is not a thing Jesus carries; it is who He is (John 1:4; 14:6).
- **IN ME** — whoever has the Son has this life now, not one day (1 John 5:12).
- **THROUGH ME** — life in you must come out of you, in words and in ways (John 7:38).

**The line that separates this from a motivational talk:** *Breath keeps you existing. Jesus makes you living.* The Greek of John's Gospel makes the point for us: *bios* is the life you can measure, *zōē* is the life of God — and John uses *zōē* every time.

### The arc — five movements, one rising river

The month's picture is **Ezekiel 47**: water flowing from God's house, rising from ankles to knees to loins to a river you must swim in — and "every thing shall live whither the river cometh" (47:9). Each week the water rises on the set board.

## MOVEMENT ONE — IN HIM WAS LIFE

**Mon 2 – Sat 7 November · River: ankles · Outcome: I HAVE LIFE · Saturday: Week 1 Wrap**

This week answers one question: *where does life come from?* Not from food, money or success — from a Person. Everything else in November depends on the listener owning this.

### EP 01 · Monday 2 — "Breath or Life?" (series intro)

> **INTRO HOOK:** A plastic plant and a real plant side by side. The presenter waters both, then pulls a leaf off each. One tears; one just bends back. *"Both are green. Only one is alive."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Breath keeps you existing. Jesus makes you living."

**Scripture — 1 John 5:11–12 (KJV)**

> **11** And this is the record, that God hath given to us eternal life, and this life is in his Son. **12** He that hath the Son hath life; and he that hath not the Son of God hath not life.

1. **Existing is not the same as living.** The New Testament has two words for life. *Bios* is the life a doctor can measure: heartbeat, breath, height. *Zōē* is God's own life, the kind that never runs out — and John uses *zōē* every time he speaks of Jesus. You can have a strong heartbeat and still be empty inside. That is existing. Jesus came to give the second kind.
2. **Life is not something Jesus carries — it is who He is.** A delivery man brings a parcel and leaves. Jesus does not hand you life and walk away; He says, "I am the life" (John 14:6). So receiving life is never about getting a thing. It is about getting Him.
3. **Have the Son, have life.** John makes it as simple as a light switch: have the Son, have life; no Son, no life. There is no third option. This whole month is about the one Person who is Life, and making sure every listener has Him.

- **Mirrors:** Genesis 2:7 (God breathed and man became a living soul) · 1 John 5:12
- **Life on your lips:** "I have the Son, so I have life."
- **Challenge:** teach the three-finger definition to one adult tonight.

### EP 02 · Tuesday 3 — "In the Beginning Was Life"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A single mango seed in the presenter's palm. *"Every mango this tree will ever grow is already inside this seed. Before the world began, everything life would ever be was already inside Jesus."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Before anything was, Life already was — in Him."

**Scripture — John 1:1–4 (KJV)**

> **1** In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. **2** The same was in the beginning with God. **3** All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made. **4** In him was life; and the life was the light of men.

1. **Before anything existed, Life already was.** Before the sun, the sea or the first tree, "in the beginning was the Word." Jesus did not begin in Bethlehem; He was already there before the beginning. Life is older than the universe, because Life is a Person who has always been.
2. **Everything that lives was made by Him.** "All things were made by him." Every mango tree, every bird, every child in Nigeria came from His hands. The One who designed life is the One who knows how to mend it — so when something in your life is broken, go to the Maker.
3. **His life is the light of men.** John ties life and light together. When Jesus' life enters a person, they begin to see clearly: who God is, who they are, what is right. People without His life are not foolish; they are in the dark. Life switches on the light.

- **Mirrors:** Genesis 1:1–3 · Colossians 1:16–17
- **Life on your lips:** "The One who made life lives in me."
- **Challenge:** find three living things today and thank Jesus for making each one.

### EP 03 · Wednesday 4 — "Life in Himself"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A phone on 2% battery, then the wall socket. *"The phone needs the socket. The socket does not need the phone."*
>
> **Punch line:** "We borrow life. Jesus IS the Source."

**Scripture — John 5:26 (KJV)**

> **26** For as the Father hath life in himself; so hath he given to the Son to have life in himself.

1. **We borrow breath; He has life in Himself.** Every breath you take is given by God, moment by moment (Acts 17:25). But the Son has life *in Himself*, just as the Father does. We are lamps; He is the power station. A lamp only shines while it is plugged in.
2. **Nobody could take His life.** Jesus said, "No man taketh it from me, but I lay it down of myself" (John 10:18). Soldiers, nails and a cross did not overpower Him. He chose to give His life for us. That is love, not defeat.
3. **That is why death could not hold Him.** Peter said it "was not possible that he should be holden of it" (Acts 2:24). The grave can hold borrowed life, but not the Source. And because you are joined to Him, death does not get the final word over you either.

- **Mirrors:** Exodus 3:2–14 (the bush that burned and was not used up — "I AM") · Acts 2:24
- **Life on your lips:** "My life is connected to the Source that never runs out."
- **Challenge:** notice everything you plugged in today. Ask: what am I plugged into?

### EP 04 · Thursday 5 — "The Life Was the Light"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A completely dark studio. Silence. One torch clicks on. *"I didn't fight the darkness. I didn't shout at it. I just switched on the light."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Where Life walks in, darkness walks out."

**Scripture — John 1:4–5 (KJV)**

> **4** In him was life; and the life was the light of men. **5** And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.

1. **Where life comes, light comes.** When Jesus walked into Zacchaeus' house, Zacchaeus suddenly saw his own greed and gave half his goods to the poor (Luke 19:8). Jesus' life does not just make you feel good; it shows you the truth.
2. **Darkness cannot overcome the light.** "The darkness comprehended it not" — it could not grasp it or put it out. Darkness has no power of its own; it is only the absence of light. There is no darkness in your home, your school or your country strong enough to put out the light of Jesus.
3. **You beat darkness by switching on the light.** You don't fight a dark room with a broom. A child carrying Jesus' life doesn't need to shout at what is wrong: kindness, truth and prayer switch on the light. Jesus told His followers, "Ye are the light of the world" (Matthew 5:14).

- **Mirrors:** Psalm 36:9 ("with thee is the fountain of life: in thy light shall we see light") · 2 Corinthians 4:6
- **Life on your lips:** "His life in me is light around me."
- **Challenge:** do one kind thing in a place that feels dark — a sad friend, a quarrel at home.

### EP 05 · Friday 6 — "Life You Can Touch"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A child reads a note from Daddy: "I love you." Then Daddy walks in and hugs them. *"The note was good. The hug was better."*
>
> **Punch line:** "God didn't send a message about life. He sent Life."

**Scripture — 1 John 1:1–2 (KJV)**

> **1** That which was from the beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our eyes, which we have looked upon, and our hands have handled, of the Word of life; **2** (For the life was manifested, and we have seen it, and bear witness, and shew unto you that eternal life, which was with the Father, and was manifested unto us;)

1. **God didn't send a message about life — He sent Life.** For centuries the prophets carried God's words. Then "the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us" (John 1:14). God came in a body you could see. Life became a Person you could walk beside.
2. **People heard, saw and touched Life.** John says "our hands have handled" Him. Jesus touched lepers nobody would touch, took children in His arms and blessed them (Mark 10:16), and let a sick woman touch His garment. Life in Jesus was close, warm and reachable.
3. **Now people should see His life in us.** Paul says the life of Jesus should be "made manifest in our body" (2 Corinthians 4:10). You may be the only Bible some of your friends ever read. When your hands help and your words heal, people touch Life through you.

- **Mirrors:** Exodus 33:18–20 (Moses could not see God's face) · John 1:14
- **Life on your lips:** "Someone will meet Jesus through me today."
- **Challenge:** let your hands give life — help someone without being asked.

### EP 06 · Saturday 7 — WEEK 1 WRAP: "Have the Son, Have Life" (about 5 min)

> **1 John 5:12** He that hath the Son hath life; and he that hath not the Son of God hath not life.
>
> **Romans 6:23** For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

**The week in five lines:**

3. Wed — We borrow life. Jesus IS the Source.
4. Thu — Where Life walks in, darkness walks out.

- **Bring it home:** All that life is in one Person, and He is a gift, not a wage (Romans 6:23). "He that hath the Son hath life" — *hath*, now, not someday. **Clear invitation to receive Christ.**
- **Declaration:** "I have the Son, so I have life."
- **Weekend challenge:** tell someone the day you received Jesus — or let today be that day.

## MOVEMENT TWO — HIS WORD IS LIFE

**Mon 9 – Sat 14 November · River: knees · Outcome: I SPEAK LIFE · Saturday: Week 2 Wrap**

If life is in Him, then what comes out of Him carries it. This week moves from His words to ours: you can only speak life if you have first stored His words.

### EP 07 · Monday 9 — "Words That Are Spirit and Life"

> **INTRO HOOK:** Two sealed jars on the table, identical. One label says LIFE, the other POISON. *"From the outside, they look the same. What matters is what's inside. Words are jars."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Words are containers. His carry life."

**Scripture — John 6:63 (KJV)**

> **63** It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

1. **Words are containers.** A word carries whatever is inside the speaker. When an angry person speaks, anger comes out; when a loving parent speaks, love comes out. Words are never empty.
2. **Jesus' words carry what He is.** "It is the spirit that quickeneth" — that makes alive. Because life is in Jesus, life is in what He says. When He said, "Lazarus, come forth," that word carried enough life to raise the dead.
3. **Reading His Word is eating, not homework.** In this same chapter Jesus calls Himself bread. You don't eat once a month and expect to stay strong; you eat every day. A few verses read slowly each day feed the life inside you.

- **Mirrors:** Genesis 1:3 (God said, and it was) · Hebrews 4:12
- **Life on your lips:** "His words in me are life in me."
- **Challenge:** read John 6 slowly this week, a few verses a night.

### EP 08 · Tuesday 10 — "To Whom Shall We Go?"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A room of children. One by one they get up and walk out. One child stays seated. The presenter kneels beside them: *"Why are you staying?"* Child: *"Where else would I go?"*
>
> **Punch line:** "There is no second source of life."

**Scripture — John 6:66–68 (KJV)**

> **66** From that time many of his disciples went back, and walked no more with him. **67** Then said Jesus unto the twelve, Will ye also go away? **68** Then Simon Peter answered him, Lord, to whom shall we go? thou hast the words of eternal life.

1. **Many walked away when it got hard.** After a hard teaching, "many of his disciples went back, and walked no more with him." Following Jesus is not always popular or easy, and some people leave when it stops being exciting.
2. **Peter stayed because of whose words they were.** Peter didn't say, "I understand everything." He said, "Thou hast the words of eternal life." He stayed for the Person, not because every question was answered.
3. **There is no second source.** "To whom shall we go?" Money, friends, fame and phones — none of them has the words of eternal life. When you don't understand, stay close and keep listening. Understanding comes to those who stay.

- **Mirrors:** Ruth 1:16 ("whither thou goest, I will go") · John 6:68
- **Life on your lips:** "Lord, You have the words of eternal life. I'm staying."
- **Challenge:** write down one Bible verse you don't understand and ask a parent or leader about it.

### EP 09 · Wednesday 11 — "Hear and Live"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A noisy room — music, chatter, a blender. The presenter says one child's name quietly. The child turns. *"In all that noise, you heard your name."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Hear Him — and live."

**Scripture — John 5:24–25 (KJV)**

> **24** Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life. **25** Verily, verily, I say unto you, The hour is coming, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God: and they that hear shall live.

1. **Hearing is the door life comes through.** "He that heareth my word, and believeth… hath everlasting life." Life does not enter because you are clever or good enough. It enters through your ears and your faith.
2. **The dead shall hear — and live.** Jesus says even the dead will hear His voice, and those who hear will live. That is how powerful His voice is: it reaches places nothing else can reach.
3. **What you hear every day decides what grows in you.** Whatever you listen to most — songs, videos, people — shapes you. Plant His Word in your ears every day, and life grows in you.

- **Mirrors:** Ezekiel 37:4 ("O ye dry bones, hear the word of the LORD") · Romans 10:17
- **Life on your lips:** "My ears are open to the voice that gives life."
- **Challenge:** swap 15 minutes of one screen habit for listening to the Gospel of John.

### EP 10 · Thursday 12 — "Only Speak the Word"

> **INTRO HOOK:** The presenter stands across the room and turns the TV on with a remote. *"I didn't touch it. I didn't walk over. I just sent the word."*
>
> **Punch line:** "His word works where His hands aren't."

**Scripture — John 4:49–50, 53; Matthew 8:8 (KJV)**

> **John 4:49** The nobleman saith unto him, Sir, come down ere my child die. **50** Jesus saith unto him, Go thy way; thy son liveth. And the man believed the word that Jesus had spoken unto him, and he went his way. … **53** So the father knew that it was at the same hour, in the which Jesus said unto him, Thy son liveth: and himself believed, and his whole house.
>
> **Matthew 8:8** The centurion answered and said, Lord, I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof: but speak the word only, and my servant shall be healed.

1. **His word works at a distance.** The nobleman begged Jesus to come to Capernaum. Jesus didn't travel; He spoke. Distance is no problem for His word — a sick relative in another city is still within its reach.
2. **Believe before you see.** "The man believed the word… and he went his way." He walked home with nothing but a promise. Faith is taking Jesus at His word before the evidence arrives.
3. **The word was "Thy son liveth" — and at that very hour, he did.** When the servants met him on the road, the fever had left "at the same hour" Jesus spoke. The word and the result arrived together, and his whole household believed.

- **Mirrors:** Psalm 107:20 ("He sent his word, and healed them") · John 4:50
- **Life on your lips:** "I believe His word before I see it."
- **Challenge:** pick one promise and say it every morning until Saturday.

### EP 11 · Friday 13 — "Death and Life Are in Your Tongue"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A child squeezes a whole tube of toothpaste onto a plate, then is asked to put it back. They can't. *"Words are like that. Once they're out, they're out."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Your tongue is a tap. Choose what flows."

**Scripture — Proverbs 18:21; Ephesians 4:29 (KJV)**

> **Proverbs 18:21** Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.
>
> **Ephesians 4:29** Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers.

1. **Your mouth is a tap.** Whatever is in the tank comes out of the tap. If the tank is full of anger, anger flows; if it is full of His Word, life flows. Jesus said, "Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh" (Matthew 12:34).
2. **Kill-words and life-words.** "You're useless." "You'll never change." Kill-words wound people for years. Life-words — "You can do it," "God is with you," "I'm sorry" — build them up. Paul says speak only what will "minister grace unto the hearers."
3. **Speaking life is not pretending.** It is not saying "there is no problem." It is saying what God says about the problem. Speaking life is agreeing with God out loud.

- **Mirrors:** Proverbs 18:21 · James 3:9–10
- **Life on your lips:** say one life-word to the person you find hardest to be kind to.
- **Challenge:** catch one kill-word today and replace it out loud.

## MOVEMENT THREE — HIS WAY IS LIFE

**Mon 16 – Sat 21 November · River: loins · Outcome: I LIVE LIFE · Saturday: Week 3 Wrap**

You cannot have His life and refuse His way. This week turns belief into a walk — what living life looks like on a Tuesday at school or at home.

### EP 13 · Monday 16 — "The Way, the Truth and the Life"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A blindfolded child is handed a paper map — they're stuck. Then the presenter takes their hand and walks them across the room. *"The map gave you directions. I gave you me."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Jesus doesn't give directions. He IS the direction."

**Scripture — John 14:1, 5–6 (KJV)**

> **1** Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me. … **5** Thomas saith unto him, Lord, we know not whither thou goest; and how can we know the way? **6** Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

1. **Jesus doesn't give directions — He is the direction.** Thomas asked, "How can we know the way?" Jesus didn't hand him a map; He said, "I am the way." The way to God is not a list of instructions. It is a Person, and if you have Jesus, you are already on it.
2. **The Way, the Truth and the Life come together.** You cannot take His life and refuse His way, or claim His truth and ignore His life, because all three are one Person. Many people want the blessing without the direction. Jesus offers Himself whole.
3. **Following Him means staying close.** Following Jesus is not keeping a rulebook from a distance. It is walking beside Someone, talking with Him, and doing what He says because you trust Him. That is why the chapter opens with "Let not your heart be troubled": the way is safe when He is the way.

- **Mirrors:** Isaiah 30:21 ("This is the way, walk ye in it") · John 14:6
- **Life on your lips:** "Jesus, You are my way. I'll walk with You."
- **Challenge:** before one decision today, ask, "Which way would Jesus walk?"

### EP 14 · Tuesday 17 — "The Narrow Gate Leads to Life"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A crowd of children squeezes through a wide doorway, laughing. One child turns sideways and slips through a narrow gap in the set. Behind the narrow gap: an open, green space. *"Most people went that way. Only one found this."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Popular isn't the same as alive."

**Scripture — Matthew 7:13–14 (KJV)**

> **13** Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat: **14** Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

1. **Popular is not the same as alive.** "Wide is the gate… and many there be which go in thereat." A crowd is not proof of the right road. Many choose what is easy and what everyone is doing, and Jesus warns that road ends in destruction.
2. **Narrow at the gate, wide inside.** The gate is narrow: you go through it with Jesus and leave some things behind. But inside, He promises you will "go in and out, and find pasture" (John 10:9). A narrow gate opens onto abundant life.
3. **Few find it — be one of the few, and bring others.** Choosing Jesus may sometimes mean standing alone in your class or your group of friends. But the few who find the way become guides for the many.

- **Mirrors:** Psalm 1:1–2 · Matthew 7:14
- **Life on your lips:** "I choose the way that leads to life."
- **Challenge:** say no to one "everybody's doing it" this week.

### EP 15 · Wednesday 18 — "Life More Abundantly"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A "thief" snatches a child's single orange and runs off. The "shepherd" walks in with a basket overflowing with oranges and fills the child's arms. *"One came to take. One came to give."*
>
> **Punch line:** "The thief takes. The Shepherd overflows."

**Scripture — John 10:10–11, 27–28 (KJV)**

> **10** The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly. **11** I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep. … **27** My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me: **28** And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.

1. **The thief takes; the Shepherd gives.** The thief comes "to steal, and to kill, and to destroy": he steals joy, kills hope and destroys families. Jesus came for the opposite reason — "that they might have life." Learn to tell which one is at work in any situation.
2. **Abundant means overflowing.** Abundant life is not having the most money or the newest phone. It is a life so full of God's peace, joy and love that it spills onto everyone around you. The Good Shepherd gave His own life to make it possible.
3. **Abundance belongs to sheep who stay near the Shepherd's voice.** "My sheep hear my voice… and they follow me." The sheep the thief catches are the ones that wander off. Stay close, and "neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand."

- **Mirrors:** Psalm 23 · John 10:10
- **Life on your lips:** "I have life, and more than enough to share."
- **Challenge:** name one thing the thief tried to steal from you this year and give it back to Jesus in prayer.

1. **The old you was buried.** "We are buried with him… into death." In Christ, the old life of lying, anger and sin went into the grave with Jesus. You don't dig up what God has buried.
2. **New life is a walk, not a jump.** "Even so we also should walk in newness of life." New life shows up in daily steps: a new way of speaking at home, of treating classmates, of using your phone. One new step a day.
3. **Leave the grave clothes behind.** Lazarus came out of the tomb still wrapped, and Jesus said, "Loose him." Old habits are grave clothes — they belong to the dead you, not the living you. "Old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

- **Mirrors:** Exodus 14 (Israel walked out of Egypt through the sea and didn't go back) · Romans 6:4
- **Life on your lips:** "Old things are passed away. I walk in newness of life."

### EP 17 · Friday 20 — "The Light of Life"

3. **Those who follow the Light become light.** Jesus promises "the light of life" — light you carry with you. He also said, "Ye are the light of the world" (Matthew 5:14). The closer you follow Him, the brighter you shine for others.

## MOVEMENT FOUR — HIS PERSON IS LIFE

**Mon 23 – Sat 28 November · River: waters to swim in · Outcome: I DECLARE LIFE · Saturday: Week 4 Wrap**

The summit of the month. Walk through John 11 across the week: Jesus does not merely perform resurrection — He IS the Resurrection. He does not merely give life — He IS the Life. Then the listeners learn to speak that life into dead places.

### EP 19 · Monday 23 — "Lord, If Thou Hadst Been Here"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A child waits at the school gate, checking a watch. Everyone else has been picked up. Then: car headlights, and Mummy's voice — *"I'm here."* Presenter: *"Martha said 'if.' Jesus was about to say 'I AM.'"*
>
> **Punch line:** "Jesus is never late — He's on His way."

**Scripture — John 11:3–6, 21 (KJV)** · read 11:1–21 on air

> **3** Therefore his sisters sent unto him, saying, Lord, behold, he whom thou lovest is sick. **4** When Jesus heard that, he said, This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of God, that the Son of God might be glorified thereby. **5** Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus. **6** When he had heard therefore that he was sick, he abode two days still in the same place where he was. … **21** Then said Martha unto Jesus, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died.

1. **Jesus loved them — and still waited.** Verse 5 says He loved Martha, Mary and Lazarus; verse 6 says He stayed where He was two more days. The delay was not because He didn't care. Love and waiting can sit side by side. Delay is not denial.
2. **Both sisters said "if."** "Lord, if thou hadst been here…" "If" lives in the past, in what could have been. Many of us live there too: if Daddy had a job, if we had money, if that hadn't happened.
3. **Jesus answers "if" with "I AM."** From the start He said, "This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of God." He was on His way to show them something bigger than a healing. When Jesus arrives, "if" turns into "I AM."

- **Mirrors:** Habakkuk 2:3 ("though it tarry, wait for it") · John 11:5–6
- **Life on your lips:** "Jesus is never late. He is on His way."
- **Challenge:** write down one prayer that feels late. Keep it for Saturday.

### EP 20 · Tuesday 24 — "I AM the Resurrection and the Life"

> **INTRO HOOK:** The presenter holds a calendar with a sticky note: SOMEDAY. Rips it off, and writes on a new one: TODAY. *"Martha said 'one day.' Jesus said, 'I am' — right now."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Resurrection isn't a day. It's a Person."

**Scripture — John 11:23–27 (KJV)**

> **23** Jesus saith unto her, Thy brother shall rise again. **24** Martha saith unto him, I know that he shall rise again in the resurrection at the last day. **25** Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live: **26** And whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die. Believest thou this? **27** She saith unto him, Yea, Lord: I believe that thou art the Christ, the Son of God, which should come into the world.

1. **Martha believed in "someday."** She believed in resurrection "at the last day." That was true, but far away. Many people believe God can do great things someday, somewhere, for someone else.
2. **Jesus said "I am" — now.** He didn't say, "I will do a resurrection." He said, "I am the resurrection." Resurrection is not a date on a calendar; it is a Person standing in front of her. Wherever Jesus is, resurrection power is present today.
3. **"Believest thou this?"** The same question comes to every listener. Martha answered, "Yea, Lord: I believe," before the stone moved and before Lazarus walked out. Our answer to that question changes everything.

- **Mirrors:** Exodus 3:14 ("I AM THAT I AM") · John 11:25–26
- **Pause moment:** stop on "Believest thou this?" and ask the audience to answer out loud.
- **Life on your lips:** "Yes, Lord. I believe You are the Resurrection and the Life."
- **Challenge:** ask three people, "Do you believe this?" and listen to their answers.

### EP 21 · Wednesday 25 — "Jesus Wept"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A child sits alone, sad. The presenter sits down beside them and says nothing — a full five seconds of silence on camera. Then: *"Sometimes the first thing love does is stay."*
>
> **Punch line:** "The Life cries with you before He calls you out."

**Scripture — John 11:32–36 (KJV)**

> **32** Then when Mary was come where Jesus was, and saw him, she fell down at his feet, saying unto him, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not died. **33** When Jesus therefore saw her weeping, and the Jews also weeping which came with her, he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled, **34** And said, Where have ye laid him? They said unto him, Lord, come and see. **35** Jesus wept. **36** Then said the Jews, Behold how he loved him!

1. **The Life feels your pain.** Jesus "groaned in the spirit, and was troubled." He didn't stand at a distance giving instructions; He stepped into their grief. We have a High Priest who is "touched with the feeling of our infirmities" (Hebrews 4:15).
2. **Tears come before triumph.** Jesus knew that in a few minutes He would raise Lazarus — and He still wept. Tears do not mean your faith is weak. The Life cries with you before He calls you out.
3. **People who carry His life carry His compassion.** The crowd said, "Behold how he loved him!" When we carry His life, people should see how we love: sitting with the sad and weeping with those who weep (Romans 12:15).

- **Mirrors:** Psalm 56:8 (He keeps our tears) · Hebrews 4:15
- **Life on your lips:** "Jesus feels what I feel, and He is not finished."
- **Challenge:** sit with someone who is sad. Don't fix it — just stay.

### EP 22 · Thursday 26 — "Take Away the Stone"

> **INTRO HOOK:** A big cardboard "stone" blocks a doorway. Three children roll it away on cue. A voice calls a child's name from off-screen; a child wrapped in loose cloth walks out, and the others unwrap them.
>
> **Punch line:** "You roll the stone. He raises the dead. Then you untie the living."

**Scripture — John 11:39–41, 43–44 (KJV)**

> **39** Jesus said, Take ye away the stone. Martha, the sister of him that was dead, saith unto him, Lord, by this time he stinketh: for he hath been dead four days. **40** Jesus saith unto her, Said I not unto thee, that, if thou wouldest believe, thou shouldest see the glory of God? **41** Then they took away the stone from the place where the dead was laid. … **43** And when he thus had spoken, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth. **44** And he that was dead came forth, bound hand and foot with graveclothes: and his face was bound about with a napkin. Jesus saith unto them, Loose him, and let him go.

1. **Our part: roll away the stone.** "Take ye away the stone." Jesus could have moved it with a word, but He gave the people a part to play. Martha objected — "he stinketh." Obeying sometimes means opening up something that smells: confessing a wrong, forgiving someone, facing what we have hidden.
2. **His part: He calls the dead by name.** "Lazarus, come forth." Only Jesus can raise the dead, and He calls by name. He did not shout at the whole graveyard; He called one man. He knows your name too.
3. **Our part again: untie the living.** Lazarus came out still bound, and Jesus told the people, "Loose him, and let him go." People who have just found new life need friends to help free them from old habits, shame and fear.

- **Mirrors:** 2 Kings 4:32–35 (Elisha and the Shunammite's son) · John 11:43–44
- **Life on your lips:** "Every stone over my life, roll away in Jesus' name."
- **Challenge:** help untie someone — forgive, include or encourage one person who is "bound."

### EP 23 · Friday 27 — "Can These Bones Live?"

> **INTRO HOOK:** Dry sticks laid on the floor in the shape of a skeleton. The presenter kneels — and instead of praying over them, speaks to them: *"Sticks, hear the word of the Lord."* The children laugh. Then the presenter turns to camera: *"That's exactly what God told Ezekiel to do."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Don't just pray about dry bones. Speak to them."

**Scripture — Ezekiel 37:3–5, 7, 10 (KJV)** · read 37:1–10 on air

> **3** And he said unto me, Son of man, can these bones live? And I answered, O Lord GOD, thou knowest. **4** Again he said unto me, Prophesy upon these bones, and say unto them, O ye dry bones, hear the word of the LORD. **5** Thus saith the Lord GOD unto these bones; Behold, I will cause breath to enter into you, and ye shall live: … **7** So I prophesied as I was commanded: and as I prophesied, there was a noise, and behold a shaking, and the bones came together, bone to his bone. … **10** So I prophesied as he commanded me, and the breath came into them, and they lived, and stood up upon their feet, an exceeding great army.

1. **God asks about something completely dead.** The bones were "very many" and "very dry" (37:2). "Can these bones live?" Ezekiel answered honestly: "O Lord GOD, thou knowest." Some situations look just as dead — a broken family, a failing school, a struggling nation.
2. **Speak to them, not only about them.** God said, "Prophesy upon these bones." Ezekiel did not only pray about the bones; he spoke God's word to them. Declaring life means speaking God's word straight into the dead situation.
3. **When he spoke, life came.** First a noise, then a shaking, then bones coming together — and finally breath and "an exceeding great army." God works through declared words; He "calleth those things which be not as though they were" (Romans 4:17). In His name, we do the same.

- **Mirrors:** Ezekiel 37:10 · Romans 4:17 (God "calleth those things which be not as though they were")
- **Life on your lips (extended):** declare life together over a family, a school, a friend — and Nigeria. "Dry bones, hear the word of the LORD. Live!"
- **Challenge:** choose one "dry bones" situation and speak a life verse over it every day until Monday.

### EP 25 · Monday 30 — "Everything Shall Live Where the River Goes"

> **INTRO HOOK:** The blue river fabric that has risen on the board all month is pulled free. Children carry it across the floor, out of the studio door and into the street. The camera follows. *"The river was never meant to stay on the board."*
>
> **Punch line:** "Everything lives where the river goes — and the river flows through you."

**Scripture — Ezekiel 47:9; John 7:38; Colossians 3:4; Galatians 2:20 (KJV)**

> **Ezekiel 47:9** And it shall come to pass, that every thing that liveth, which moveth, whithersoever the rivers shall come, shall live: and there shall be a very great multitude of fish, because these waters shall come thither: for they shall be healed; and every thing shall live whither the river cometh.
>
> **John 7:38** He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.
>
> **Colossians 3:4** When Christ, who is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory.
>
> **Galatians 2:20** I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.

1. **Christ is your life — not a part of it.** Paul says "Christ, who is our life," not "Christ, who is part of our life." He is not a Sunday slot or a hobby. "I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me." School, family and dreams all flow from Him.
2. **The river flows out of you.** "Out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water." Life given to you is meant to flow through you. The Dead Sea takes water in but has no outlet, so it stays dead; in Ezekiel's vision, the river flows into it and heals it (47:8). Life you keep goes stale; life you give away multiplies.
3. **Wherever the river goes, things live.** "Every thing shall live whither the river cometh." That means your home, your class, your street and Nigeria. Kids Inspiring Nation means children carrying Life into the nation, one person at a time.

- **Mirrors:** Ezekiel 47:9 · John 7:37–38

- **The full declaration, standing:** "I have the Son, so I have life. I speak life. I live life. I declare life. And everywhere I go, I transform lives."

## VERSION B — Cinematic animation (Flow) · the DP's cut

The whole trailer is one visual idea: **thirst becomes flood.** Three rules carry it:

- **Sell it in three seconds** with sound before picture, motion toward the lens, and a sudden jump in scale.
- **Change something every two or three seconds:** a new lens, a new light, a new sound. The eye never gets the chance to leave.
- **Let colour tell the story.** There is no saturated colour in the film until the first drop of water falls.

### The first three seconds

| Time | What happens | Why it hooks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00.0 | Black. A sharp, dry **CRACK**. | Sound before picture makes the viewer lean in to see what made it. |
| 0:00.5 | Extreme macro: a fissure splits across red clay, racing straight at the lens. | Motion toward the viewer triggers instinct. Nobody scrolls past something coming at them. |
| 0:02.0 | Hard cut to a vast aerial: an endless dry riverbed with one tiny boy alone on the bank. | The jump in scale poses a question — *who is he, and why is he alone?* — and people stay for the answer. |
| 0:01.0 onward | The narrator, under it all: "You can be breathing…" | The voice arrives just as the eye is caught. |
