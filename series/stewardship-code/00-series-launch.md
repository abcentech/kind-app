Series Introduction

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:13

> “No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or
> else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.”
> — Luke 16:13 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Money must never become the master of our hearts. God gives us money as a tool to manage,
deploy and use for His purposes.

### Teaching Point 1: Everyone Serves a Master

Jesus did not say that having money is wrong. He warned that serving money is wrong.

A master is anything that controls your choices, commands your loyalty and determines your
priorities.

Money becomes a master when:

- You disobey God to obtain it.
- You lie to keep it.
- You measure your worth by it.
- You cannot release it when God directs you to give.
- You place financial gain above righteousness.

The first question in stewardship is not, “How much money do I have?”

It is, “Who rules my heart?”

### Teaching Point 2: Money Was Created to Serve

Money can be used to:

- Buy food.
- Pay school fees.
- Support families.
- Build businesses.
- Spread the gospel.
- Educate children.
- Solve community problems.
- Create opportunities.

Money is powerful, but it must remain in its proper position.

It must remain in your hand and never sit on the throne of your heart.

### Teaching Point 3: Stewardship Is Management Under God

A steward is not the final owner. A steward manages what belongs to someone else.

God owns everything, but He entrusts different portions of His resources to different
people.

He expects us to manage those resources:

- Faithfully
- Wisely
- Honestly
- Productively
- Generously
- Eternally

### Illustration

Imagine a powerful car without a driver.

The car may have speed, strength and value, but without someone controlling it, it can cause
destruction.

Money is like that car.

It can take you forward when properly directed, but it can destroy lives when it is allowed
to control the driver.

You must remain in the driver’s seat under the direction of Jesus Christ.

### Stewardship Challenge

Write down your honest answers to these three questions:

- What do I currently believe money can do for me?
- What financial pressure most easily controls my emotions?
- How can I begin making money serve God’s purpose?

### Prayer

money a useful servant. In Jesus’ name. Amen.
