### Main Scripture

#### Matthew 25:21

> “His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful

> lord.”
> — Matthew 25:21 (KJV)

### Central Truth

The final goal of stewardship is to hear Jesus Christ say, “Well done, good and faithful
servant.”

### Teaching Point 1: The Faithful Steward Recognises Ownership

The faithful steward understands:

- God owns everything.
- God is the Source.
- Every resource carries an assignment.
- Access is not absolute ownership.
- Every steward will give an account.

This understanding produces humility and responsibility.

### Teaching Point 2: The Faithful Steward Manages and Multiplies

The faithful steward:

- Records resources
- Plans before spending
- Remains honest
- Protects what belongs to others
- Develops gifts
- Solves problems
- Saves consistently
- Invests wisely
- Produces value

Faithfulness is active, not passive.

### Teaching Point 3: The Faithful Steward Serves Christ

The greatest test is not how much money you possess.

It is whether Jesus Christ remains your Master.

A faithful steward can possess wealth without being possessed by wealth.

The order remains:

- Christ is my Master.
- I am His steward.
- Money is my servant.
- People are more valuable than possessions.
- Eternity guides my decisions.

#### Luke 19:17

> little, have thou authority over ten cities.”

### Illustration

A servant may carry the king’s treasure.

The treasure may pass through the servant’s hands, but the servant must never mistake
himself for the king.

Wealth may pass through your hands, but it must never sit on the throne of your heart.

### The Complete Stewardship Code

#### The Ownership Code

God owns it; I manage it.

#### The Faithfulness Code

I must manage little before receiving more.

#### The Mastery Code

Christ is my Master; money is my servant.

#### The Multiplication Code

I must develop, grow and deploy what God puts in my hands.

#### The Eternal Code

I will give an account and invest for eternal reward.
