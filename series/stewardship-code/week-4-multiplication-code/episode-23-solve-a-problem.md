# EPISODE 23 — THURSDAY, AUGUST 27
## SOLVE A PROBLEM

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Thursday, August 27

---

### Main Scripture

#### Genesis 41:39

> “And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, Forasmuch as God hath shewed thee all this, there is none so
> discreet and wise as thou art.”
> — Genesis 41:39 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Value is created when wisdom, skills and resources are used to solve genuine problems.

### Teaching Point 1: Joseph Saw More Than a Crisis

Egypt faced a future famine.

Joseph did not merely identify the problem. He presented:

- A plan
- A system
- A storage strategy
- A leadership structure
- A long-term solution

His ability to solve the problem created opportunity and influence.

### Teaching Point 2: Problems Can Become Opportunities to Serve

Around you, people may struggle with:

- Difficult school subjects
- Technology
- Transportation
- Organisation
- Cleaning
- Design
- Communication
- Access to useful information

A steward asks, “What can I do to help?”

### Teaching Point 3: Income Often Follows Value

People normally exchange money for something they value.

That value may be:

- A product
- A service
- Convenience
- Knowledge
- Entertainment
- Safety
- Time saved
- A problem solved

Do not only ask, “How can I make money?”

Also ask, “What valuable problem can I solve?”

### Illustration

A student notices that younger children struggle with mathematics.

The student organises simple revision lessons, prepares worksheets and helps the children
improve.

Knowledge has become a solution. The solution may also become a source of responsible
income.

### Stewardship Challenge

Write down three problems around you.

For each problem, answer:

- Who experiences it?
- What causes it?
- What skill could help?
- What small solution can I test?
