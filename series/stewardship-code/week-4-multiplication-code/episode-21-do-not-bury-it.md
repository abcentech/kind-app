# EPISODE 21 — TUESDAY, AUGUST 25
## DO NOT BURY IT

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Tuesday, August 25

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 19:20

> “And another came, saying, Lord, behold, here is thy pound, which I have kept laid up in a
> napkin.”
> — Luke 19:20 (KJV)

### Central Truth

God does not give us gifts merely to preserve them. He expects us to develop and deploy
them.

### Teaching Point 1: Preservation Is Not Always Faithfulness

The servant returned exactly what he received.

Nothing was stolen, but nothing was multiplied.

He protected the resource from loss but also prevented it from producing value.

Playing safe can become disobedience when God has instructed us to act.

### Teaching Point 2: Fear Buries Gifts

People may hide their abilities because they fear:

- Failure
- Criticism
- Embarrassment
- Comparison
- Rejection
- Imperfection

Fear may keep a gift safe, but it also keeps the gift unproductive.

### Teaching Point 3: Gifts Grow Through Use

A gift develops when you:

- Practise
- Learn
- Receive correction
- Attempt difficult tasks
- Serve others
- Repeat consistently

Unused ability remains undeveloped potential.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### 2 Timothy 1:6

> “Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by
> the putting on of my hands.”
> — 2 Timothy 1:6 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Proverbs 18:16

> “A man’s gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men.”
> — Proverbs 18:16 (KJV)

### Illustration

A musical instrument may be beautifully designed and extremely expensive.

But if it remains permanently inside its case, it produces no music.

Your gifts must come out of hiding.

### Stewardship Challenge

Choose one gift or skill you have neglected.

Practise it for at least thirty minutes and share the result with a trusted person.

### Prayer

Father, deliver me from fear. Help me stir up, develop and deploy every gift You have placed
within me. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I will not bury what God expects me to build.

### Transition

A gift becomes increasingly valuable when it is intentionally developed.
