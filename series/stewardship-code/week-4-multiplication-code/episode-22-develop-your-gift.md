# EPISODE 22 — WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 26
## DEVELOP YOUR GIFT

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Wednesday, August 26

---

### Main Scripture

#### Proverbs 22:29

> “Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand
> before mean men.”
> — Proverbs 22:29 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Natural ability becomes valuable through diligence, training and consistent improvement.

### Teaching Point 1: Potential Is Only a Beginning

Potential means you could become good at something.

It does not mean you have already developed mastery.

Potential must be followed by:

- Practice
- Study
- Discipline
- Feedback
- Experience
- Improvement

### Teaching Point 2: Diligence Creates Distinction

Diligence means giving careful, consistent effort.

Diligent teenagers:

- Arrive prepared
- Practise basics
- Ask questions
- Correct mistakes
- Meet deadlines
- Continue learning

Excellence is often the accumulated result of small acts of diligence.

### Teaching Point 3: Skill Creates Opportunity

People are often willing to pay for:

- Useful knowledge
- Reliable service
- Creative ability
- Technical skill
- Clear communication
- Problem-solving capacity

Developing a gift can create opportunities for service, influence and income.

### Illustration

A naturally talented footballer may initially perform better than others.

But if the talented player refuses training while another player practises daily, the
disciplined player may eventually become far better.

Talent gives a beginning. Diligence produces growth.

### Stewardship Challenge

Create a seven-day development plan for one skill.

Include:

- What you will practise
- How long you will practise
- What resource you will study
- Who will give feedback
- What result you hope to produce
