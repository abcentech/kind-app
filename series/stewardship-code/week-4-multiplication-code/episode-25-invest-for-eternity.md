# EPISODE 25 — SATURDAY, AUGUST 29
## INVEST FOR ETERNITY

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Saturday, August 29

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:9

> “And I say unto you, Make to yourselves friends of the mammon of unrighteousness; that, when
> ye fail, they may receive you into everlasting habitations.”
> — Luke 16:9 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Temporary resources should be used in ways that produce lasting and eternal impact.

### Teaching Point 1: Money Is Temporary

Money can:

- Be spent
- Be lost
- Lose value
- Be stolen
- Become outdated
- Remain behind when life ends

Because money is temporary, it should be directed towards things of lasting value.

### Teaching Point 2: People Have Eternal Value

Using resources to build people can produce long-term impact.

You can invest in people through:

- Education
- Mentoring
- Encouragement
- Food
- Training
- Gospel outreach
- Medical care
- Community development

The greatest return is not always measured in money.

### Teaching Point 3: Kingdom Investment Sends Treasure Ahead

Jesus taught us to lay up treasure in heaven.

Kingdom investment includes:

- Supporting the gospel
- Helping the vulnerable
- Serving people
- Giving generously
- Using gifts for God’s glory
- Building lives
- Obeying God with resources

These investments produce rewards that outlive earthly possessions.

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### 2 Corinthians 4:18

> “While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for
> the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal.”
> — 2 Corinthians 4:18 (KJV)

### Illustration

An expensive outfit may look impressive for a season, but it will eventually wear out or
become unfashionable.

The same amount invested in a child’s education, spiritual growth or useful skill may
influence an entire lifetime.

### Weekly Review

Multiplication means:

- Working productively
- Refusing to bury gifts
- Developing skills
- Solving problems
- Saving consistently
- Investing wisely
- Building people
- Creating eternal impact

- One problem I will solve
