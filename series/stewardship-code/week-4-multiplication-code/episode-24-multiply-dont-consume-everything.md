# EPISODE 24 — FRIDAY, AUGUST 28
## MULTIPLY, DON’T CONSUME EVERYTHING

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Friday, August 28

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 19:16

> “Then came the first, saying, Lord, thy pound hath gained ten pounds.”
> — Luke 19:16 (KJV)

### Central Truth

A steward preserves and multiplies part of today’s resources instead of consuming everything
immediately.

### Teaching Point 1: Consumption Uses Resources Today

Consumption is necessary.

We must spend on:

- Food
- Clothing
- Transport
- Education
- Responsibilities
- Appropriate enjoyment

The problem begins when everything is consumed and nothing is preserved.

### Teaching Point 2: Saving Preserves Resources

Saving means setting aside part of today’s income for future use.

Saving can prepare you for:

- Emergencies
- Education
- Business tools
- Opportunities
- Future purchases
- Investments
- Giving

Saving gives your future self resources.

### Teaching Point 3: Investment Seeks Responsible Growth

Investment places resources into something that may produce future value.

Teenagers can begin by investing in:

- Books
- Courses
- Skills
- Tools
- Small projects
- Productive equipment
- Carefully supervised financial instruments

Investment should involve learning, patience and wise guidance.

### Supporting Scripture 1

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Proverbs 6:6–8

> “Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise:
> — Proverbs 6:6–8 (KJV)

> Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler,

> Provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest.”

### Illustration

Two teenagers receive the same holiday income.

The first spends everything on entertainment and fashion.

The second enjoys some, saves some and buys tools to begin a simple service.

They started equally, but their stewardship created different futures.

### Stewardship Challenge

For your next income, create a multiplication allocation:

- Give
- Save
- Spend
- Invest
- Learn

Set aside the saving and investment portions before spending.

### Prayer

Father, deliver me from consuming everything. Give me wisdom to save, invest and multiply
responsibly. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I am a creator and multiplier, not merely a consumer.

### Transition

The highest purpose of multiplication is not personal accumulation. It is eternal impact.
