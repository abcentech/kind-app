# EPISODE 20 — MONDAY, AUGUST 24
## OCCUPY UNTIL HE COMES

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Monday, August 24

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 19:13

> “And he called his ten servants, and delivered them ten pounds, and said unto them, Occupy
> till I come.”
> — Luke 19:13 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Jesus expects His servants to remain spiritually faithful and productively engaged until He
returns.

### Teaching Point 1: Occupy Means Engage Productively

The servants were not told to sit beside the money and wait.

They were instructed to put it to work.

Christian faith should produce:

- Diligence
- Creativity
- Responsibility
- Service
- Enterprise
- Productivity

Waiting for Christ is not an excuse for laziness.

### Teaching Point 2: Work Can Honour God

Work is not merely a way to receive money.

Work can:

- Serve people
- Develop character
- Create solutions
- Build communities
- Express excellence
- Honour God

Productive work is part of stewardship.

### Teaching Point 3: Prayer and Action Belong Together

Prayer is essential, but prayer should lead us into obedient action.

We should:

- Pray for wisdom.
- Plan carefully.
- Learn diligently.
- Work honestly.
- Review results.
- Improve continuously.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### Ecclesiastes 9:10

> “Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device,
> nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.”
> — Ecclesiastes 9:10 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Colossians 3:23

> “And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men.”
> — Colossians 3:23 (KJV)

### Illustration

A farmer cannot pray over an empty field and expect a harvest without planting seed.

Prayer invites God’s wisdom and help. Work puts that wisdom into action.

### Stewardship Challenge

Spend at least thirty minutes developing a productive skill today.

### Prayer

Lord, make me productive while I wait for Your return. Deliver me from laziness and teach me
to work heartily as unto You. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I will pray, plan, work and produce.
