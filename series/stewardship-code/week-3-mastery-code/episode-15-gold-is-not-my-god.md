# EPISODE 15 — TUESDAY, AUGUST 18
## GOLD IS NOT MY GOD

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Tuesday, August 18

---

### Main Scripture

#### 1 Timothy 6:10

> “For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have
> erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.”
> — 1 Timothy 6:10 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Money itself is not evil, but loving money can corrupt faith, character and relationships.

### Teaching Point 1: The Bible Condemns the Love of Money

The verse does not say that money is the root of all evil.

It warns against the love of money.

The love of money appears when people:

- Crave wealth above righteousness
- Measure everyone by possessions
- Use people to gain profit
- Refuse contentment
- Abandon faith for financial advantage

### Teaching Point 2: Money Makes a Poor God

Money cannot:

- Forgive sin
- Give eternal life
- Heal every disease
- Guarantee peace
- Create genuine love
- Replace family
- Satisfy the soul

Money can solve financial problems, but it cannot carry the weight of worship.

### Teaching Point 3: Enjoy Resources Without Worshipping Them

God may bless people with resources.

The correct response is to:

- Give thanks
- Remain humble
- Share generously
- Use wisely
- Refuse pride
- Maintain devotion to Christ

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### Psalm 62:10

> “Trust not in oppression, and become not vain in robbery: if riches increase, set not your
> heart upon them.”
> — Psalm 62:10 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Hebrews 13:5

> “Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have:
> for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.”
> — Hebrews 13:5 (KJV)

### Illustration

Fire is valuable when it remains in a fireplace or cooker.

It can provide warmth and prepare food.

But when fire takes over the house, it destroys what it was meant to serve.

Money must remain in its proper place.

### Stewardship Challenge

List three things money cannot give you.

Then list three ways money can be used responsibly.

### Prayer

Father, cleanse my heart from the love of money. Help me enjoy Your provision without
worshipping possessions. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> Gold is not my god.
> Jesus Christ is my Lord.

### Transition
