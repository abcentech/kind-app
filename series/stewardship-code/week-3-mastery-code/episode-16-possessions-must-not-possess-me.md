# EPISODE 16 — WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 19
## POSSESSIONS MUST NOT POSSESS ME

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Wednesday, August 19

---

### Main Scripture

#### Mark 10:22

> “And he was sad at that saying, and went away grieved: for he had great possessions.”
> — Mark 10:22 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Possessions become dangerous when they control our identity, confidence and obedience.

### Teaching Point 1: The Rich Young Ruler Owned Much

The young man had many possessions.

His wealth was not the only problem.

The deeper issue was that he could not release what stood between him and obedience to
Jesus.

What he owned had gained control over his heart.

### Teaching Point 2: Identity Must Come From Christ

Teenagers may be tempted to build identity around:

- Phones
- Shoes
- Clothes
- Cars
- Schools
- Neighbourhoods
- Social media appearance

These things can be enjoyed, but they must never determine personal worth.

### Teaching Point 3: Attachment Is Revealed by Release

You may not know how attached you are to something until you are asked to:

- Share it
- Give it
- Delay it
- Lose it
- Live without it
- Choose obedience over it

A steward can release possessions because identity and security are found in God.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### Matthew 6:21

> “For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.”
> — Matthew 6:21 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Luke 12:15

> “And he said unto them, Take heed, and beware of covetousness: for a man’s life consisteth
> not in the abundance of the things which he possesseth.”
> — Luke 12:15 (KJV)

### Illustration

A teenager feels confident only when wearing a particular brand of shoe.

Without it, the teenager feels inferior.

The shoe is no longer merely being worn. It has started controlling the teenager’s sense of
worth.

### Stewardship Challenge

Choose one possession you value highly.

Write:

- Why do I value it?
- How would I feel without it?
- Does it affect how I see myself?
- Could I share or release it if God required it?

### Prayer

Father, let my identity remain rooted in Christ. Deliver me from allowing possessions,
brands or appearances to control my confidence. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I possess things.
> Things do not possess me.
