# EPISODE 19 — SATURDAY, AUGUST 22
## BREAK MAMMON’S CHAINS

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Saturday, August 22

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:13

> “No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or
> else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.”
> — Luke 16:13 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Financial freedom begins when Jesus Christ is Master and money is deliberately kept in the
position of a servant.

### Teaching Point 1: Mammon Chains Through Disobedience

Money is becoming a master when you are willing to:

- Lie for it
- Cheat for it
- Steal for it
- Abandon worship for it
- Betray trust for it
- Hurt people for it

No amount of money is worth losing obedience to Christ.

### Teaching Point 2: Mammon Chains Through Identity

Money is becoming a master when your confidence rises and falls according to:

- Your possessions
- Your family’s status
- Your clothes
- Your phone
- Your ability to impress people

Your identity must be anchored in Christ.

### Teaching Point 3: Mammon Chains Through Fear

Money may control people by making them constantly afraid of:

- Not having enough
- Losing what they possess
- Being seen as poor
- Missing an opportunity
- Helping others
- Trusting God

Wise planning is good. Constant financial fear is another form of bondage.

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Proverbs 11:28

> “He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the righteous shall flourish as a branch.”
> — Proverbs 11:28 (KJV)

### Illustration

A prisoner may be connected to a chain made of gold.

The chain may look expensive, but it is still a chain.

Wealth, possessions and status can appear attractive while still keeping a heart in bondage.

### Weekly Review

#### Signs money may be becoming a master:

- You disobey God to gain it.
- You compare yourself because of it.
- You lie or cheat to keep it.
- You cannot give any of it away.
- You build your identity around possessions.
- You live in constant fear of losing it.

### Stewardship Challenge

Practise one act of financial freedom:

- Give something.
- Delay a purchase.
- Reject peer pressure.
- Admit a dishonest action.
- Help someone.
- Save instead of showing off.
- Thank God for what you already possess.

### Prayer

Jesus Christ, break every chain of mammon in my heart. Deliver me from greed, fear,
comparison and material pride. Remain my Master, and teach me to direct money as Your
steward. Amen.

### Weekly Declaration

> Mammon cannot chain me.
> Riches cannot claim me.
> Silver cannot crown me.
> My identity is in Christ.
> Jesus Christ is my Master.
> Money is my servant.

### Transition to Week Four

Once money is placed under Christ’s authority, it can be productively multiplied and used
for eternal impact.
