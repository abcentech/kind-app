# EPISODE 14 — MONDAY, AUGUST 17
## TWO MASTERS

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Monday, August 17

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:13

> “No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or
> else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.”
> — Luke 16:13 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Jesus Christ must remain the only Master of our hearts. Money must remain a servant.

### Teaching Point 1: A Master Demands Loyalty

A master has the right to command.

If money is your master, it will command you to:

- Chase it at any cost
- Compromise your values
- Compare yourself with others
- Fear constantly
- Ignore people
- Disobey God

Jesus deserves complete loyalty.

### Teaching Point 2: Mammon Is More Than Currency

Mammon represents the power, confidence and identity people attach to money.

Money becomes mammon when you believe:

- Money is your ultimate security.
- Money determines your value.
- Money can solve every problem.
- Life has no meaning without wealth.
- God’s instructions can be ignored for profit.

### Teaching Point 3: You Must Choose

Jesus did not present serving God and mammon as a comfortable partnership.

One must remain above the other.

The Christian order is:

- Christ is Master.
- I am His steward.
- Money is my servant.

### Illustration

Two people cannot safely hold and turn the steering wheel of the same moving car in opposite
directions.

There must be one recognised driver.

There must also be one recognised Master in your life.

### Stewardship Challenge

Complete this sentence honestly:

“Money most often tries to control me through __________.”

Then write one biblical response to that pressure.

### Prayer

Jesus Christ, remain the only Master of my heart. Deliver me from every form of loyalty to
mammon. Let money remain beneath Your authority. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> Jesus Christ alone is my Master.

### Transition

Money becomes dangerous when it takes the place that belongs to God.
