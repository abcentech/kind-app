# EPISODE 17 — THURSDAY, AUGUST 20
## NEEDS, WANTS AND PRESSURE

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Thursday, August 20

---

### Main Scripture

#### Philippians 4:11

> “Not that I speak in respect of want: for I have learned, in whatsoever state I am,
> therewith to be content.”
> — Philippians 4:11 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Contentment gives us the strength to separate genuine needs from wants created by pressure.

### Teaching Point 1: Needs and Wants Are Different

A need is something necessary for health, safety, responsibility or basic functioning.

A want is something desirable but not essential.

A want is not automatically wrong, but calling every want a need creates poor financial
decisions.

### Teaching Point 2: Pressure Can Distort Desire

Pressure may come from:

- Friends
- Advertisements
- Influencers
- Trends
- Fear of missing out
- Comparison
- The desire to impress

Pressure can turn a useful desire into an emotional emergency.

### Teaching Point 3: Contentment Is Learned

Paul said, “I have learned.”

Contentment does not mean:

- Refusing to grow
- Rejecting improvement
- Avoiding ambition
- Remaining careless

Contentment means you can be grateful and peaceful while working responsibly for
improvement.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### 1 Timothy 6:6

> “But godliness with contentment is great gain.”
> — 1 Timothy 6:6 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Hebrews 13:5

> “Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have:
> for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.”
> — Hebrews 13:5 (KJV)

### Illustration

A teenager has a phone that performs every necessary function.

After seeing friends with newer phones, the teenager begins to feel that the present phone
is useless.

The device did not suddenly stop working. Comparison changed the teenager’s feelings.

### Stewardship Challenge

For every purchase you consider today, classify it as:

- Need
- Useful want
- Pressure-driven want

Delay any pressure-driven purchase for at least seven days.

### Prayer

to recognise needs, wants and pressure. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I will not spend to impress people.
> My identity is secure in Christ.

### Transition

Money stops controlling us when we deliberately give it a godly assignment.
