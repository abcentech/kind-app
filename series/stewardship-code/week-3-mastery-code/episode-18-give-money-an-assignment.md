# EPISODE 18 — FRIDAY, AUGUST 21
## GIVE MONEY AN ASSIGNMENT

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Friday, August 21

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:9

> “And I say unto you, Make to yourselves friends of the mammon of unrighteousness; that, when
> ye fail, they may receive you into everlasting habitations.”
> — Luke 16:9 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Money becomes a servant when it is deliberately directed towards meaningful and eternal
purposes.

### Teaching Point 1: Money Is a Tool

A tool cannot decide what it will build.

The person holding the tool gives it direction.

Money can be directed to:

- Feed
- Educate
- Build
- Support
- Create
- Invest
- Give
- Solve

Money should receive instructions from a wise steward.

### Teaching Point 2: Earning Should Lead to Impact

The goal of work is not only personal consumption.

Biblical earning can provide resources to:

- Meet personal responsibilities
- Help family
- Give to others
- Support Kingdom work
- Build solutions
- Create jobs
- Develop skills

Income should become impact.

### Teaching Point 3: Give Before Money Controls You

Generosity breaks the illusion that money must be held at all costs.

Giving reminds the heart:

- God is my Source.
- Money is not my security.
- People matter.
- I can release resources.
- I am a steward, not an owner.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### Ephesians 4:28

> “Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the
> thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth.”
> — Ephesians 4:28 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Proverbs 19:17

> “He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD; and that which he hath given will he
> pay him again.”
> — Proverbs 19:17 (KJV)

### Illustration

A teenager uses part of a holiday income to purchase books for a younger student who cannot
afford them.

The money is no longer merely currency. It has become an instrument of education and hope.

### Stewardship Challenge

Give part of your money, time or skill to meet one genuine need today.

Do it quietly and intentionally.

### Prayer

Father, teach me to give money a godly assignment. Let my resources solve problems, build
people and advance Your purposes. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> My money must serve God’s mission.
