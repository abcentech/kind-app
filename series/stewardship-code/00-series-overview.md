## THE SEVEN STEWARDSHIP CODE PRINCIPLES

- God owns everything.
- I am only a steward.
- Every steward will give an account.
- Faithfulness comes before increase.
- Money is a tool, not a master.
- God rewards multiplication.
- Invest today for eternal rewards.

## MONTHLY DECLARATION

> Gold is not my god;
> Mammon cannot chain me.
> Riches cannot claim me;
> Silver cannot crown me.

> Jesus Christ, my Master;
> I am His faithful steward.
> Yahweh owns it all;
> Shrewdly, I will serve.

> God owns everything.
> I am only a steward.
> I will give an account.
> I will be faithful before increase.
> Money will remain a tool, never my master.
> I will multiply what God gives me.
> I will invest today for eternal rewards.

> Christ my Master!
> Money my servant!

## The Four Codes

| Week | Code | Sub-theme | Primary Passage | Weekly Question |
|------|------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **THE OWNERSHIP CODE** | God Owns It; I Manage It | Luke 16:1–2 | Whose money, time, talents and opportunities am I managing? |
| 2 | **THE FAITHFULNESS CODE** | Manage Little Before Asking for More | Luke 16:10–12 | Can God trust me with what is already in my hands? |
| 3 | **THE MASTERY CODE** | Christ My Master, Money My Servant | Luke 16:9–13 | Do I control money, or has money begun to control me? |
| 4 | **THE MULTIPLICATION CODE** | Build It, Grow It, Send It Ahead | Luke 19:11–26 | What am I doing to increase the value of what God has placed in my hands? |
