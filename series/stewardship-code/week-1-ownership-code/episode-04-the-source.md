# EPISODE 4 — WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 5
## THE SOURCE

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Wednesday, August 5

---

### Main Scripture

#### Deuteronomy 8:18

> “But thou shalt remember the LORD thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get
> wealth, that he may establish his covenant which he sware unto thy fathers, as it is this
> day.”
> — Deuteronomy 8:18 (KJV)

### Central Truth

God is the Source. Money, jobs, businesses and opportunities are channels through which His
provision may come.

### Teaching Point 1: God Gives the Power to Produce

Money does not normally fall from heaven.

It may come through:

- Work
- Business
- Creativity
- Skill
- Investment
- Gifts
- Opportunities

But behind every legitimate channel is the God who gives:

- Life
- Health
- Intelligence
- Strength
- Favour
- Ideas
- Relationships
- Opportunity

Never worship the channel and forget the Source.

### Teaching Point 2: The Source Is Greater Than the Resource

A resource can finish.

A job may end. A customer may leave. A gift may be spent. An opportunity may change.

But God remains.

When your trust is in God, you can remain peaceful even when one channel of provision
changes.

### Teaching Point 3: Remembering God Produces Humility

Success can make people say:

- “I did it all by myself.”
- “Nobody helped me.”
- “I do not need anyone.”
- “My intelligence gave me everything.”

The Bible tells us to remember the Lord because remembrance protects us from pride.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### James 1:17

> lights, with whom is no variableness, neither shadow of turning.”
> — James 1:17 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### 1 Chronicles 29:14

> “But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly after
> this sort? for all things come of thee, and of thine own have we given thee.”
> — 1 Chronicles 29:14 (KJV)

### Illustration

A child receives ₦5,000 from a parent and uses ₦500 to buy the parent a birthday gift.

The gift is meaningful, but the money originally came from the parent.

In the same way, everything we offer to God first came from Him.

### Stewardship Challenge

Write down three channels through which God currently provides for you.

Beside each one, write:

“God is my Source; this is only a channel.”

### Prayer

Father, help me remember You in every season of success. Keep me humble and grateful. Let my
confidence remain in You and not merely in money or people. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> God is my Source.
> Money is only a resource.
