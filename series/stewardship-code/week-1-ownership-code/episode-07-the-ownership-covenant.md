# EPISODE 7 — SATURDAY, AUGUST 8
## THE OWNERSHIP COVENANT

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Saturday, August 8

---

### Main Scripture

#### 1 Chronicles 29:14

> “But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly after
> this sort? for all things come of thee, and of thine own have we given thee.”
> — 1 Chronicles 29:14 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Everything belongs to God, and everything in our hands must be managed as a sacred trust.

### Teaching Point 1: God Is the Owner

This week, we learned that God owns:

- The world
- Silver and gold
- Our abilities
- Our opportunities
- Our time
- Our lives

God is not asking to become part-owner. He already owns everything.

### Teaching Point 2: I Am the Steward

A steward receives:

- Resources
- Responsibility
- Instructions
- Opportunity

A steward must respond with faithfulness.

The question is not merely, “What do I have?”

The better question is, “How am I managing what belongs to God?”

### Teaching Point 3: My Life Is an Assignment

Your life is not an accident.

Your abilities, environment, relationships and opportunities can become tools through which
God’s purpose is fulfilled.

Stewardship means turning what God has given you into service, value and impact.

### Supporting Scripture 1

> — 1 Corinthians 4:2 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Psalm 24:1

> “The earth is the LORD’S, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.”

### Illustration

A trusted courier may carry a package worth millions of naira.

The package may be in the courier’s hands, but it does not belong to the courier.

The courier’s success is measured by whether the package reaches the right destination in
the right condition.

Your success as a steward is measured by whether God’s resources fulfil God’s assignment
through your life.

### Weekly Review

#### Who owns everything?

God.

#### Who am I?

A steward.

#### What must I do?

Manage faithfully.

#### Why has God given me resources?

To fulfil an assignment.

#### What will I eventually do?

Give an account.

### Stewardship Challenge

Create a Personal Resource Inventory with these headings:

- Time
- Money
- Gifts
- Knowledge
- Devices
- Relationships
- Opportunities
- Influence

Beside each one, write one way you will manage it more faithfully.

### Weekly Covenant

> Everything I am and everything I possess belongs to God.
> I am not the ultimate owner. I am His steward.
> I will discover the purpose of every resource He places in my hands.
> I will manage faithfully, use wisely and live ready to give an account.
