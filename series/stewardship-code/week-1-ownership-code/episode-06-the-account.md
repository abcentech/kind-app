# EPISODE 6 — FRIDAY, AUGUST 7
## THE ACCOUNT

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Friday, August 7

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:2

> “And he called him, and said unto him, How is it that I hear this of thee? give an account
> of thy stewardship; for thou mayest be no longer steward.”
> — Luke 16:2 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Every steward will eventually explain what was done with what was received.

### Teaching Point 1: God Keeps Records

The steward’s management was reviewed.

In the same way, God sees:

- How we use our time
- How we handle money
- How we treat people
- How we develop our abilities
- How we respond to opportunities
- How we use our influence

Nothing entrusted to us is outside His concern.

### Teaching Point 2: Accountability Improves Responsibility

Knowing that an assignment will be graded changes how a serious student approaches it.

In the same way, knowing that we will give an account should help us:

- Plan better
- Waste less
- Obey faster
- Work diligently
- Remain honest
- Live intentionally

### Teaching Point 3: Accountability Is Personal

Each person must answer for his or her own stewardship.

You cannot blame:

- Your friends
- Your parents
- Your school
- Your siblings
- Social media
- Your background

These things may influence you, but you must still make responsible choices.

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### 2 Corinthians 5:10

### Illustration

A student who knows there will be an examination does not wait until the examiner enters the
room before beginning to learn.

The coming examination influences today’s preparation.

Eternal accountability should influence today’s stewardship.

### Stewardship Challenge

Review how you used the following during the past seven days:

- Time
- Money
- Internet access
- Talents
- Opportunities

Write one improvement beside each area.
