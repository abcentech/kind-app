# EPISODE 3 — TUESDAY, AUGUST 4
## THE STEWARD

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Tuesday, August 4

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:1

> “And he said also unto his disciples, There was a certain rich man, which had a steward; and
> the same was accused unto him that he had wasted his goods.”
> — Luke 16:1 (KJV)

### Central Truth

A steward manages another person’s resources according to the owner’s instructions.

### Teaching Point 1: A Steward Is a Manager

The steward in Luke 16 was responsible for the rich man’s property.

He did not own the resources, but he had:

- Access
- Authority
- Opportunity
- Responsibility

Being a steward means God allows us to handle resources that still belong to Him.

### Teaching Point 2: Access Is Not Ownership

Having access to something does not mean you can use it carelessly.

You may have access to:

- Your parents’ money
- A family car
- School equipment
- Church property
- Internet access
- A leadership position
- Another person’s trust

Access creates responsibility. It does not remove accountability.

### Teaching Point 3: A Steward Must Be Faithful

The most important quality of a steward is not popularity, intelligence or wealth.

It is faithfulness.

Faithfulness means:

- Doing what is right consistently.
- Protecting what has been entrusted.
- Following instructions.
- Producing the expected result.
- Remaining honest when nobody is watching.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### 1 Corinthians 4:2

> “Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.”
> — 1 Corinthians 4:2 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### 1 Peter 4:10

> “As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good
> stewards of the manifold grace of God.”
> — 1 Peter 4:10 (KJV)

### Illustration

A school prefect has authority, but the prefect does not own the school.

The prefect cannot invent personal rules merely to impress friends. The authority was given
to serve the school’s purpose.

In the same way, God gives us resources and influence to serve His purposes, not merely to
please ourselves.

### Stewardship Challenge

Choose one resource from yesterday’s inventory and answer:

- Why has God placed this in my hands?
- Who can it help?
- How can I manage it better?
