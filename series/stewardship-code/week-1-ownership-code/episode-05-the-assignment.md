# EPISODE 5 — THURSDAY, AUGUST 6
## THE ASSIGNMENT

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Thursday, August 6

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 19:13

> “And he called his ten servants, and delivered them ten pounds, and said unto them, Occupy
> till I come.”
> — Luke 19:13 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Every resource God gives carries an assignment. We must discover what it was given to
accomplish.

### Teaching Point 1: God Gives Resources for a Reason

The master did not give the servants money merely to admire or display.

He expected them to use it productively.

God gives resources so they can:

- Meet needs
- Build capacity
- Create value
- Bless people
- Support families
- Advance His Kingdom
- Produce a responsible increase

### Teaching Point 2: Every Resource Has Potential

A phone can become:

- A distraction
- A classroom
- A camera
- A business tool
- A Bible
- A design studio
- A communication platform

The object may be the same, but the assignment determines how it is used.

### Teaching Point 3: Ask Purpose Before Pleasure

Before using a resource, ask:

- Why did God allow me to have this?
- What good can it accomplish?
- What problem can it solve?
- Who can it help?
- How can it honour God?

Pleasure is not automatically sinful, but purpose must lead pleasure.

### Illustration

Two teenagers receive identical smartphones.

One uses the phone mainly for endless scrolling and entertainment.

The other learns video editing, designs flyers, studies courses and helps a church media
team.

The difference is not the device. The difference is purpose.

### Stewardship Challenge

Select one resource you own and write three productive assignments it can fulfil.

### Prayer

valuable resources only for temporary pleasure. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration
