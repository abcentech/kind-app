## THE OWNER

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Monday, August 3

---

### Main Scripture

#### Psalm 24:1

> “The earth is the LORD’S, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.”
> — Psalm 24:1 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Everything belongs to God. Stewardship begins when we stop acting like absolute owners and
begin living as responsible managers.

### Teaching Point 1: God Owns the World

The earth did not create itself, and we did not bring ourselves into existence.

God created:

- The land
- The oceans
- The minerals
- The plants
- The animals
- Human intelligence
- Human strength
- Every opportunity for productivity

Therefore, God is not merely one participant in the economy. He is the ultimate Owner of
everything within it.

### Teaching Point 2: God Owns What Is in Your Hands

Your phone may have your name on it. Your parents may have purchased it. But the materials,
intelligence, health and opportunity that made the purchase possible came from God.

God owns:

- Your time
- Your abilities
- Your education
- Your possessions
- Your opportunities
- Your future
- Your influence

You are responsible for how these resources are used.

### Teaching Point 3: Ownership Changes Behaviour

When you understand that something belongs to God, you treat it differently.

You will be less likely to:

- Waste it.
- Destroy it.
- Use it for sin.
- Neglect it.
- Compare it carelessly.
- Take it for granted.

The awareness of God’s ownership creates responsibility.

### Illustration

Suppose a friend lends you an expensive laptop for a week.

Because the laptop belongs to someone else, you would probably:

- Carry it carefully.
- Keep food and water away from it.
- Avoid dangerous downloads.
- Return it on time.
- Report any problem immediately.

You would treat it carefully because you know you are accountable to the owner.

Your life and resources should be treated with even greater care because they belong to God.
