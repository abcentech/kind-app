## Before you generate — 5 minutes of setup that saves an hour

1. **Open Flow** → new project, name it `Stewardship Code Trailer`.
2. **Model:** pick the newest **Veo** available. **Ratio:** 16:9. Generate the vertical 9:16
   pass separately rather than cropping — reframing anime crops badly.
3. **Lock your characters first.** Generate a few stills of Micah and Ada, pick the best one of
   each, and load them as **Ingredients** (reference images) for every shot they appear in.
   Without this, faces drift between shots and the trailer falls apart.
4. **Generate 3–4 variants per shot.** Assume roughly one in three is usable. Budget for it.
5. **Chain the shots with Frames to Video.** Use the last frame of shot 2 as the first frame of
   shot 3 so the chains match, and the last frame of shot 4 into shot 5. Use **Extend** if a
   shot needs another beat.
6. **No text.** Every prompt says "no text, no letters, no numbers in frame." Titles go on in
   the edit — see the brief.

## Assembly

1. Export the 6 chosen clips at the highest quality Flow offers.
2. Lay them on a **0:45** timeline in order and trim to the durations in
   [02-trailer-script.md](02-trailer-script.md): 7.5 · 7.5 · 7.0 · 8.0 · 7.5 · 7.5.
   Take the 3 seconds out of the tails, never the openings.
3. Cut on the beat: 1→2 hard cut on the chain strike · 2→3 slow dissolve as the room falls away
   · 3→4 hard cut on the first vault click · 4→5 fast **white flash** on the shatter · 5→6 a
   rising light wipe into the sunrise.
4. Voiceover on its own track, ducked music beneath it.
5. Title cards per the brief's table — gold `#B8860B` on navy `#1F3864`. The four Code cards
   must land exactly on their vault clicks.
6. **Import the captions:** [trailer-captions.srt](trailer-captions.srt) for 16:9,
   [trailer-captions-vertical.srt](trailer-captions-vertical.srt) for 9:16. Style them per the
   caption spec in the script.
7. **Grade all six clips together** so the golds match; Flow shots drift warm/cool between
   generations.
8. Export the four ratios listed in the brief. Burn captions into the social cuts; upload the
   `.srt` as a proper subtitle track on YouTube.

## If a shot won't cooperate

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Faces change between shots | You skipped the Ingredients step. Lock a reference still of each character and attach it to every shot. |
| Garbled text appears anyway | Strengthen the negative prompt and re-roll. Never try to fix text in Veo — remove it and title in the edit. |
| Chains look ugly rather than seductive | Add *"beautiful ornate jewelry-like golden chains, elegant and tempting"*. The theology matters here: mammon is attractive, not repulsive. |
| Shot 4 is chaotic mush | Cut it into two 8s generations — two rings each — and edit them together. |
| Shot 5's montage blurs into one thing | Generate it as two clips of 2–3 beats each. Veo struggles past three actions in one prompt. |
| Shot 3's giant hand looks eerie | Add *"soft, open, welcoming, made of warm light, not grasping"*. It should read as provision, never as a trap. |
| Refused or degraded output | Add *"stylized anime characters, illustrated, non-photorealistic"* and raise the apparent age to *"teenage anime protagonist"*. |
| Camera moves too fast to read | Add *"slow deliberate camera, single continuous move"* and drop one action from the prompt. Veo obeys fewer instructions better. |
