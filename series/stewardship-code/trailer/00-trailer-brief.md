## The Concept

The series is called a **Code**. So the trailer is a **vault**.

A teen sits alone with his money and his phone, and the shadows in his room resolve into a
giant lock. Golden chains — mammon — coil around his wrists. He discovers he was never the
owner. Four glowing tumblers turn, one for each weekly Code, the chains shatter into light, he
puts what he has to work, and he walks into sunrise free.

**Emotional arc:** *trapped → named → re-owned → unlocked → sent → free.*
**One-line promise:** *You are not the owner. You are the steward. And there is a code.*

## Style Bible
*Paste this block into every shot prompt, unchanged. Consistency across shots depends on it.*

> **Style:** Modern anime, cel-shaded 2D-look with subtle 3D camera depth. Clean confident
> linework, high-contrast dramatic lighting, volumetric god-rays, lens flares, fine floating
> dust motes. Cinematic anime film quality — think a high-budget theatrical anime opening.
> **Palette:** deep navy blue and midnight indigo shadows, hot molten gold highlights, a
> single accent of warm teal. **Camera:** anamorphic, shallow depth of field, deliberate and
> weighty. **Mood:** reverent, urgent, hopeful. No text or letters rendered in frame.

**Palette hex** (matches the series master document): navy `#1F3864` · blue `#2E75B6` ·
gold `#B8860B` · teal accent `#2A9D8F`

> **Policy note:** keep the prompts explicitly **stylized anime**, never "photorealistic."
> Veo restricts photoreal depictions of minors and will refuse or degrade the shot. The words
> *anime, cel-shaded, illustrated* in every prompt are doing real work — don't drop them.

## Sound Design

- **Music:** low sustained cello drone under shots 1–2 → near-silence and one high sustained
  string in shot 3 → a struck piano note on each tumbler in shot 4 → percussive build through
  shot 5 → full strings and a choral swell on the title.
- **SFX:** coin spinning on wood · phone buzz · chain links tightening (metallic, close) ·
  **four heavy vault clicks** (the signature sound of the series) · glass-shatter into chimes ·
  pencil, broom, coin, hammer cut on the beat in shot 5 · wind and distant city on the rooftop.
- **Silence is a tool.** Shot 3 nearly empties out, and all music cuts for the half-second
  before the fourth vault click.

> If you use Veo's native audio, keep it to ambience and SFX only and lay the voiceover in
> yourself — generated speech won't match across six separate shots.
