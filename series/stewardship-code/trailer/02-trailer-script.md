## SHOT 1 — THE LOCK · 0:00.0–0:07.5

**VISUAL.** Night. MICAH sits alone on his bedroom floor, back against the bed, gold coins and
a glowing phone on the rug in front of him. The phone light is the only light on his face and
it makes him look small. He lifts his head. The camera pulls back and up — and the shadows on
the wall resolve into an enormous circular vault lock, with Micah sitting at its center like a
keyhole.

**VO** *(0:01.0)*
> Everybody serves a master.
>
> Most people never find out who theirs is —
> until money asks them to choose.

**CAPTIONS.** Cues 1–2. Bottom-centered, safe margin.
**ON SCREEN.** Nothing. Let the image work.
**AUDIO.** A coin spinning to rest on wood. One phone buzz. A low cello drone entering under it.

---

## SHOT 2 — THE CHAINS · 0:07.5–0:15.0

**VISUAL.** Micah reaches for the phone. As his hand closes on it, chains of molten gold erupt
from the coins and coil fast around both wrists, pulling taut. He strains. Fear turning to
anger. The chains are beautiful — ornate, glowing, seductive. Behind him ADA steps out of the
dark and extends an open hand, calm and certain.

**VO** *(0:08.0)*
> Money is a great servant.
>
> And a terrible god.

**CAPTIONS.** Cues 3–4. Hold the beat of silence between the two lines — don't run them together.
**ON SCREEN.** Nothing.
**AUDIO.** Chain links tightening, close and metallic. Rising drone. One held breath, then cut.

---

## SHOT 3 — THE QUESTION · 0:15.0–0:22.0
*New beat. This is the theological hinge of the whole month.*

**VISUAL.** Close on Micah's bound hands holding the phone. The camera drifts up past him — and
the room falls away into a vast starfield. The coins, the phone, the room, the whole city below
are revealed as tiny objects resting in an enormous open hand of light. Micah looks up and
understands. His face changes: not defeated — *relieved*.

**VO** *(0:15.5)*
> The first question was never "How much do I have?"
>
> It's — "Whose is it?"

**CAPTIONS.** Cues 5–6. Keep the two questions on separate cues; the contrast is the point.
**ON SCREEN.** Nothing.
**AUDIO.** Everything drops to near-silence. A single sustained high string. Distant, wide reverb.

---

## SHOT 4 — THE FOUR CODES · 0:22.0–0:30.0
*The money shot. Generate the most variants here.*

**VISUAL.** Micah takes Ada's hand. The room dissolves into a vast dark vault interior. Four
enormous concentric rings hang in the air around them. One after another, each ring rotates and
slams into alignment — four heavy locks — each firing a shockwave of gold light. On the fourth,
the chains shatter into a thousand rising embers. Micah stands to full height, freed.

**VO** *(0:22.5)*
> So there's a code.
>
> Ownership. Faithfulness. Mastery. Multiplication.

**CAPTIONS.** Cues 7–11. **Each Code name is its own cue, landing exactly on its vault click.**
This sync is the best two seconds in the trailer — get it right.
**ON SCREEN.** The four Code title cards, one per click:
`THE OWNERSHIP CODE` · `THE FAITHFULNESS CODE` · `THE MASTERY CODE` · `THE MULTIPLICATION CODE`
Gold `#B8860B`, all caps, wide tracking, lower third.
**AUDIO.** Four heavy vault clicks. A struck piano note on each. Glass shattering into wind
chimes on the fourth. **Cut the music dead for the half-second before click four.**

## SHOT 5 — PUT IT TO WORK · 0:30.0–0:37.5
*New beat. Stewardship isn't just freedom from — it's freedom for.*

**VISUAL.** A fast montage, each image a half-second, gold light trailing between them: Micah
writing in a notebook, counting honestly · Ada sweeping a shop floor, working · a single coin
pressed into another teenager's palm · Micah sketching a design, building something · a small
plant pushing up through soil in a jar on a windowsill. Every action leaves a thread of gold
light that gathers upward.

**VO** *(0:30.5)*
> Be faithful in the little.
>
> Don't bury what He put in your hands.

**CAPTIONS.** Cues 12–13.
**ON SCREEN.** Nothing — the montage is already busy.
**AUDIO.** Percussive, building. A pencil scratch, a broom, a coin ring, a hammer tap — each cut
on the beat. Strings climbing underneath.

## SHOT 6 — SUNRISE · 0:37.5–0:45.0

**VISUAL.** Dawn. Micah and Ada stand together on a city rooftop, wind moving their clothes,
skyline warm and hazy below. Micah flips a gold coin high; it catches the sunrise and spins in
slow motion. He catches it and closes it firmly in his fist — a decision, not a grab. Both look
up and out. The camera rises and pulls back into a wide silhouette against an enormous rising
sun, with generous empty sky above them.

**VO** *(0:38.0)*
> Christ my Master. Money my servant.
>
> Twenty-six episodes. All of August.
>
> **Crack the code.**

**CAPTIONS.** Cues 14–16.
**ON SCREEN.** *(into the empty sky — this is why the shot is framed wide)*
- 0:40.0 — **THE STEWARDSHIP CODE** *(large, gold)*
- 0:41.0 — *Principles of Managing Money* *(italic, white, beneath)*
- 0:43.0 — `SUBSCRIBE ON ALL PLATFORMS` *(small, on a dark pill)* then
  **@KidsInspiringNation** *(gold, larger)*

> **As built** (the delivered read is 37.4s, not 45 — see `video/README.md` for the real
> timings): the subtitle follows the VOICE, which says *Principles*, not the *Secrets* this
> script originally specified. The `26 EPISODES · ALL AUGUST` line was dropped for the

- 0:44.5 — fade to navy `#1F3864`

**AUDIO.** Rooftop wind, distant city. The coin rings as it's caught. Full strings and choir
resolving to one clean sustained note. Let it ring into the fade.

## Caption specification

| Property | 16:9 | 9:16 |
|----------|------|------|
| Max characters per line | 42 | 26 |
| Max lines per cue | 2 | 2 |
| Position | Bottom centre, 10% safe margin | Centre, upper-middle third |
| Font | Heavy geometric sans, all caps | Same |
| Size | 4.5% of frame height | 5.5% of frame height |
| Fill | White `#FFFFFF` | White `#FFFFFF` |
| The Code names (cues 8–11) | Gold `#B8860B` | Gold `#B8860B` |
| Background | 55% black rounded box, or a soft drop shadow | Same — never plain text over sky |
| Minimum duration | 1.0s per cue | 1.0s per cue |

**Why the vertical file is separate:** 9:16 crops the sides, so 42-character lines break badly
and captions collide with the title cards. Use `trailer-captions-vertical.srt` — same words,
shorter lines, higher placement.

> **Burn captions in for the social cuts.** Most feed views are muted. Keep a clean uncaptioned
> master for YouTube, where the `.srt` gets uploaded as a proper subtitle track instead.

## Voiceover direction

A young adult voice — a mentor, an older sibling. **Not** an announcer, not a hype voice.

- Low, warm, unhurried. Trust the silence between lines.
- **"And a terrible god"** — drop the volume, don't raise it. Menace is quiet.
- **"Whose is it?"** — genuine question, not rhetorical. Let it hang.
- The four Code names — flat, even, inevitable. Like tumblers falling.
- **"Crack the code"** — the only line with lift in it. Land it and stop.

**Total VO: 86 words over 45 seconds.** That's deliberately slow. If your read finishes early,
you are reading it right.
