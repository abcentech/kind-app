# EPISODE 13 — SATURDAY, AUGUST 15
## THE FAITHFULNESS TEST

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Saturday, August 15

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:10

> “He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust
> in the least is unjust also in much.”
> — Luke 16:10 (KJV)

### Central Truth

God examines how we handle little before entrusting greater responsibility.

### Teaching Point 1: Faithfulness Is Consistent

Faithfulness means doing what is right:

- Repeatedly
- Quietly
- Correctly
- Promptly
- Honestly

It is not one impressive action followed by weeks of carelessness.

### Teaching Point 2: Faithfulness Requires Systems

Good intentions are not always enough.

Simple systems help you become faithful:

- Keep records
- Use a budget
- Save automatically
- Return borrowed items
- Set reminders
- Review spending
- Ask for accountability

Systems make responsible behaviour easier to repeat.

### Teaching Point 3: Faithfulness Prepares You for Increase

The goal is not merely to receive more money.

Faithfulness prepares you for:

- Greater trust
- Greater influence
- Greater opportunities
- Greater responsibility
- Greater impact

God’s promotion is larger than financial increase.

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Proverbs 28:20

> “A faithful man shall abound with blessings: but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not

### Illustration

Brushing your teeth for one hour once a month cannot replace brushing for a few minutes
every day.

Many results come through consistency, not occasional intensity.

### Weekly Review

Faithfulness means:

- Knowing what you have
- Recording what you use
- Planning before spending
- Protecting what belongs to others
- Remaining honest
- Repeating wise habits

### Stewardship Challenge

Create your four-part Faithfulness System:

- What will I record?
- What will I save?
- What will I stop wasting?
- Who will hold me accountable?

### Prayer

character for every increase You desire to place in my hands. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Weekly Declaration

> I am preparing for increase.
