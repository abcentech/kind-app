# EPISODE 11 — THURSDAY, AUGUST 13
## FAITHFUL WITH ANOTHER PERSON’S

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Thursday, August 13

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:12

> “And if ye have not been faithful in that which is another man’s, who shall give you that
> which is your own?”
> — Luke 16:12 (KJV)

### Central Truth

How you treat what belongs to others reveals whether you are ready for greater personal
responsibility.

### Teaching Point 1: Borrowed Things Are a Test

When you borrow something, you should:

- Ask permission
- Use it carefully
- Return it promptly
- Return it in good condition
- Report any damage honestly

Carelessness with borrowed property reveals poor stewardship.

### Teaching Point 2: Shared Resources Also Belong to Someone

School, family and church property may feel as though they belong to nobody.

But they belong to a community.

Examples include:

- Chairs
- Books
- Toilets
- Computers
- Sports equipment
- Musical instruments
- Electricity
- Water

Responsible people protect shared resources.

### Teaching Point 3: Trust Opens Doors

People entrust greater opportunities to those who have demonstrated responsibility.

If you are faithful with:

- Someone else’s time
- Someone else’s money
- Someone else’s idea
- Someone else’s equipment
- Someone else’s confidence

You become a person who can be trusted with more.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### Philippians 2:4

> “Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.”
> — Philippians 2:4 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Colossians 3:23

> “And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men.”
> — Colossians 3:23 (KJV)

### Illustration

Two students borrow cameras.

The first student returns the camera clean, fully charged and safely packed.

The second returns it late with a missing cover and a scratched lens.

Which student will be trusted with the more expensive camera next time?

### Stewardship Challenge

Do one of the following today:

- Return something you borrowed.
- Repair something you damaged.
- Replace something you lost.
- Clean a shared space.
- Ask forgiveness for careless handling.

### Prayer

Father, make me trustworthy with what belongs to others. Help me protect every resource
placed in my care. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I honour God by caring for what belongs to another.

### Transition

Faithfulness is not only about careful handling. It is also about integrity.
