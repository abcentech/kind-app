# EPISODE 8 — MONDAY, AUGUST 10
## FAITHFUL IN LITTLE

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Monday, August 10

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:10

> “He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust
> in the least is unjust also in much.”
> — Luke 16:10 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Little resources are not meaningless. They are tests that reveal whether we are ready for
greater responsibility.

### Teaching Point 1: Little Reveals Character

Many people say:

- “I will save when I earn more.”
- “I will give when I become rich.”
- “I will become responsible when I have a business.”
- “I will stop wasting money when I receive more.”

Jesus teaches that behaviour in little predicts behaviour in much.

### Teaching Point 2: More Does Not Repair Bad Habits

If a person wastes ₦1,000, the same person may waste ₦100,000.

A larger income does not automatically create:

- Discipline
- Wisdom
- Integrity
- Contentment
- Generosity

These qualities must be developed before increase arrives.

### Teaching Point 3: Little Is Training

Small amounts teach you how to:

- Record
- Budget
- Save
- Give
- Delay gratification
- Make choices
- Resist pressure

Do not despise the training ground.

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Matthew 25:21

> “His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful
> over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy
> lord.”
> — Matthew 25:21 (KJV)

### Illustration

A leaking bucket does not first need more water.

It needs the leak repaired.

Pouring more water into a leaking bucket only creates a larger waste.

Before asking God for more, identify and repair the leaks in your present stewardship.

### Stewardship Challenge

Identify three small resources you currently manage.

For each one, write one habit that would demonstrate faithfulness.

### Prayer

Father, help me become faithful in little. Teach me not to despise small beginnings or delay
discipline until increase comes. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> My little is not useless.
> My little is my test.

### Transition
