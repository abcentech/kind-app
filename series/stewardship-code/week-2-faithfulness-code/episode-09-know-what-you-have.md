# EPISODE 9 — TUESDAY, AUGUST 11
## KNOW WHAT YOU HAVE

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Tuesday, August 11

---

### Main Scripture

#### Proverbs 27:23

> “Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy herds.”
> — Proverbs 27:23 (KJV)

### Central Truth

You cannot manage accurately what you refuse to measure.

### Teaching Point 1: Good Stewards Pay Attention

In Bible times, flocks and herds represented wealth.

The owner had to know:

- How many animals were present
- Which ones were healthy
- Which ones were missing
- Which ones were growing
- Which ones required attention

Modern stewardship also requires awareness.

### Teaching Point 2: Records Reveal Reality

You may feel that you do not spend much, but written records may reveal otherwise.

Keeping records helps you see:

- Repeated purchases
- Wasteful habits
- Emotional spending
- Forgotten debts
- Missed savings
- Giving patterns

Facts help you make better decisions.

### Teaching Point 3: Measure More Than Money

Teenagers should also track:

- Time
- Internet use
- Screen time
- Study hours
- Skill development
- Commitments
- Personal goals

Every important resource deserves attention.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### Proverbs 4:26

> — Proverbs 4:26 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Psalm 90:12

> “So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.”
> — Psalm 90:12 (KJV)

### Illustration

Your phone can show how much data or battery each application consumes.

Without checking the report, you may blame the wrong app.

In the same way, a money record reveals which habits are consuming your resources.

### Stewardship Challenge

Track every amount you receive and spend for seven days.

Use these headings:

- Date
- Amount Received
- Amount Spent
- Purpose
- Need or Want
- Balance

### Prayer

honestly and make wise corrections. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I measure what I manage.
> I improve what I measure.
