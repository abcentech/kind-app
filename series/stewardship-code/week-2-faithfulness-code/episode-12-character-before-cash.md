# EPISODE 12 — FRIDAY, AUGUST 14
## CHARACTER BEFORE CASH

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Friday, August 14

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 16:11

> “If therefore ye have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will commit to your
> trust the true riches?”
> — Luke 16:11 (KJV)

### Central Truth

Money tests character. Integrity must be stronger than the desire for quick profit.

### Teaching Point 1: Money Reveals the Heart

Money can reveal:

- Greed
- Fear
- Dishonesty
- Pride
- Generosity
- Contentment
- Self-control

Money does not always create character problems. It often exposes what was already present.

### Teaching Point 2: Integrity Is More Valuable Than Profit

Never exchange your character for:

- Extra change
- Examination malpractice
- Internet fraud
- Stolen property
- False advertising
- Secret commissions
- Dishonest business

Money gained through corruption carries a cost greater than its value.

### Teaching Point 3: True Riches Require Trust

Jesus spoke of “true riches.”

God values things money cannot purchase:

- Wisdom
- Integrity
- Spiritual understanding
- Trust
- Good character
- Kingdom responsibility

Financial faithfulness can prepare us to carry these greater treasures.

### Supporting Scripture 1

#### Proverbs 10:9

> “He that walketh uprightly walketh surely: but he that perverteth his ways shall be known.”
> — Proverbs 10:9 (KJV)

### Supporting Scripture 2

#### Proverbs 28:6

> “Better is the poor that walketh in his uprightness, than he that is perverse in his ways,
> though he be rich.”
> — Proverbs 28:6 (KJV)

### Illustration

A cashier mistakenly gives a teenager ₦5,000 more than the correct change.

Nobody else notices.

Returning the money shows that the teenager’s integrity is worth more than temporary gain.

### Stewardship Challenge

Examine your recent financial actions.

Correct any dishonest act involving:

- Change
- Borrowing
- Selling
- School payments
- Online transactions
- Shared money

### Prayer

Lord, build my character before increasing my cash. Give me courage to remain honest even
when dishonesty appears profitable. In Jesus’ name. Amen.

### Daily Declaration

> I will never exchange integrity for income.

### Transition

Faithfulness is not one good action. It is a consistent pattern.
