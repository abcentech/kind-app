# EPISODE 10 — WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 12
## PLAN BEFORE YOU SPEND

> **Series:** The Stewardship Code — *Secrets to Managing Money* · Wednesday, August 12

---

### Main Scripture

#### Luke 14:28

> “For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the
> cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?”
> — Luke 14:28 (KJV)

### Central Truth

A budget is a plan that gives money an assignment before emotions spend it.

### Teaching Point 1: Planning Is Biblical

Jesus used the picture of a builder who first counted the cost.

Planning is not a lack of faith.

Wise planning helps you:

- Understand what is required
- Set priorities
- Prepare for future needs
- Avoid unnecessary debt
- Complete what you begin

### Teaching Point 2: A Budget Protects Priorities

Without a budget, the loudest desire may take all the money.

A simple teenage budget can include:

- Give
- Save
- Spend
- Invest
- Learn
- Help

A budget ensures that important things are not ignored.

### Teaching Point 3: A Budget Gives You Freedom

Some people think a budget is a punishment.

A good budget actually gives you permission to spend within agreed limits.

It protects you from:

- Regret
- Impulse
- Peer pressure
- Confusion
- Constant shortage

### Supporting Scripture 2

### Illustration

A football team does not enter a match with every player running wherever he feels like
running.

The team uses a formation.

A budget is a financial formation. It puts every part of your money in the right position.

### Stewardship Challenge

Create a budget for your next allowance, gift or income.

Use percentages or actual amounts under these headings:

- Giving
- Saving
- Spending
- Investing
- Learning
