# SUNDAY GUIDE 4 — Week 4 Recap: THE MULTIPLICATION CODE
> *The Stewardship Code* · Sun, Aug 30 · No new teaching today

## This Week's Journey
**THE MULTIPLICATION CODE — build it, grow it, send it ahead.** We were commissioned to
**occupy till He comes** — engage productively, not passively wait (Ep 20). We learned why
the third servant failed: **fear buries gifts** (Ep 21). We committed to **develop our
gifts**, because gifts grow through use and skill creates opportunity (Ep 22). We looked
outward to **solve a problem**, the way Joseph saw more than a crisis (Ep 23). We chose to
**multiply rather than consume everything** (Ep 24). And yesterday we learned to **invest for
eternity** — sending treasure ahead where it lasts.

Tomorrow: the Grand Finale.

## Bible Reading
Read **Luke 19:11–26** — the parable of the pounds, our second anchor passage.

*As you read, look for:* what the master actually rewarded. Not the size of the return — the
*faithfulness* of the servant. And notice the one thing he would not accept: a gift left
buried.

## Talk About It
1. What did you put to work this week that you had previously been sitting on?
2. Fear buried the third servant's pound. What is fear currently burying in you?
3. "Occupy till I come." What does productive waiting look like for a teenager this year?

## Pray Together
Father, we will not bury what You have given us. Help us develop every gift, solve real
problems and produce value that serves people and honours You. Teach us to invest today for
eternal reward — knowing the reward is Yours to give, and not our achievement to claim.
In Jesus' name. Amen.

## Declare Together
