# SUNDAY GUIDE 1 — Week 1 Recap: THE OWNERSHIP CODE
> *The Stewardship Code* · Sun, Aug 9 · No new teaching today

## This Week's Journey
**THE OWNERSHIP CODE — God owns it; I manage it.** We met **the Owner**: the earth, the
silver and the gold are all His (Ep 2). We learned who we are — **the steward**, a manager
of someone else's goods (Ep 3). We traced every ability and opportunity back to **the
Source** (Ep 4). We discovered that every resource carries an **assignment** (Ep 5), and
that one day every steward gives **an account** (Ep 6). Yesterday you signed the **Ownership
Covenant** and built your Personal Resource Inventory.

## Bible Reading
Read **Psalm 24:1–2**, then **Luke 16:1–2** together.

*As you read, look for:* the two halves of the code. Psalm 24 says who owns it. Luke 16 says
what the owner eventually asks for — *"Give an account of thy stewardship."*

## Talk About It
1. What was the hardest item on your Resource Inventory to hand back and call God's?
2. Episode 4 said God is the *Source*, not just the supplier. How does remembering the
   Source protect you from pride?
3. "Give an account of thy stewardship." If that review happened this week, what would you
   want to change first?

## Pray Together
Father, You are the Owner of our lives and everything connected to us. Deliver us from
entitlement and waste. Show us the assignment behind every resource You have placed in our
hands, and keep us ready to give an account. In Jesus' name. Amen.

## Declare Together
> God owns everything.
> I am only a steward.
> My life is an assignment.
> I will be ready to give an account.
