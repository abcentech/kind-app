# SUNDAY GUIDE 0 — Series Opening: Whose Money Is It?
> *The Stewardship Code* · Sun, Aug 2 · No new teaching today

## This Week's Journey
Yesterday we cracked open the month: **everyone serves a master**, and money is either a
useful servant or a cruel god (Ep 1). Tomorrow we begin **THE OWNERSHIP CODE** — *God owns
it; I manage it.* This week's question: *Whose money, time, talents and opportunities am I
managing?*

## Bible Reading
Read **Luke 16:1–13** — the parable of the shrewd manager, our anchor text for the whole
month.

*As you read, look for:* the word **steward**. Notice the manager never owned anything. He
was accused of wasting what belonged to someone else — and he still had to give an account.

## Talk About It
1. Jesus said you cannot serve God and mammon. Where in ordinary life does money quietly
   start asking to be served?
2. The manager was fired for *wasting*, not stealing. What's the difference — and does that
   change how you think about waste?
3. If God owns everything in your room right now, what would you handle differently
   tomorrow?

## Pray Together
Father, before we learn how to manage money, settle the question of ownership in our hearts.
You are the Owner; we are Your stewards. Let Jesus Christ be our only Master and let money
stay our servant. In Jesus' name. Amen.

## Declare Together
> Christ my Master!
> Money my servant!
