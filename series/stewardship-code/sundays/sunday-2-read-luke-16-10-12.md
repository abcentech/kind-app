# SUNDAY GUIDE 2 — Week 2 Recap: THE FAITHFULNESS CODE
> *The Stewardship Code* · Sun, Aug 16 · No new teaching today

## This Week's Journey
**THE FAITHFULNESS CODE — manage little before asking for more.** We learned that little is
not small; it is **training** (Ep 8). We started to **know what we have**, because records
reveal reality (Ep 9). We learned to **plan before we spend**, and that a budget is freedom,
not a cage (Ep 10). We were tested with **what belongs to someone else** (Ep 11), and we
chose **character before cash** (Ep 12). Yesterday's **Faithfulness Test** asked the honest
question: *can God trust me with what is already in my hands?*

## Bible Reading
Read **Luke 16:10–12** — three verses, the whole code.

*As you read, look for:* how many times "little" and "much" appear together. Faithfulness in
the least is never separate from faithfulness in much — it *is* the qualification for it.

## Talk About It
1. Where did you find a leak this week once you actually started keeping records?
2. Verse 12 is about *another person's* things. How do we treat what we borrow — and what
   does that reveal?
3. More money never repairs bad habits. Which single habit are you carrying into next week?

## Pray Together
Father, make us faithful in the little that is already in our hands. Give us honest records,
wise plans and clean character. We would rather be trustworthy than wealthy — and we trust
You for the increase in Your time. In Jesus' name. Amen.

## Declare Together
> I will be faithful before increase.
> Money will remain a tool, never my master.
