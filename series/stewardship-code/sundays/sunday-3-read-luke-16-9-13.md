# SUNDAY GUIDE 3 — Week 3 Recap: THE MASTERY CODE
> *The Stewardship Code* · Sun, Aug 23 · No new teaching today

## This Week's Journey
**THE MASTERY CODE — Christ my Master, money my servant.** We faced the choice between **two
masters** (Ep 14). We said out loud that **gold is not my god**, because the love of money —
not money itself — is the root of evil (Ep 15). We refused to let **possessions possess us**
(Ep 16). We learned to tell the difference between **needs, wants and pressure** (Ep 17), and
to **give money an assignment** before it invents one for us (Ep 18). Yesterday we named the
three chains of mammon — disobedience, identity and fear — and broke them.

## Bible Reading
Read **Luke 16:9–13**, then **1 Timothy 6:6–10**.

*As you read, look for:* what Jesus tells us to *do* with money in verse 9 — use it to bless
people and serve eternal purposes — right before He says in verse 13 that we cannot serve it.

## Talk About It
1. Which chain has the strongest grip in your life right now — disobedience, identity or
   fear?
2. Contentment is *learned*, not automatic. What did you practise this week that helped?
3. Name one purchase you almost made because of pressure. What did you decide instead?

## Pray Together
Father, deliver us from greed, comparison and the fear of lack. Let our identity come from
Christ and never from what we own. Break every chain mammon has tried to put on us. Jesus,
be our only Master. In Jesus' name. Amen.

## Declare Together
> Gold is not my god;
> Mammon cannot chain me.
> Riches cannot claim me;
> Silver cannot crown me.
