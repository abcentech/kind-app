# SHORT 09 — KNOW WHAT YOU HAVE
> Companion to Episode 9 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Your phone can tell you exactly which app drained your battery. Can you say
the same about your money?

**📖 Scripture:** "Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy
herds." — Proverbs 27:23

**💡 Truth:** You cannot manage accurately what you refuse to measure. The shepherd
counted his flock — which were healthy, which were missing. You may *feel* like you don't
spend much; written records tell the truth about repeated purchases and emotional
spending. And money isn't the only flock. Time, screen hours and study hours all deserve
the same attention.

**🚀 Challenge:** For seven days, write down everything you receive and spend — date,
amount, purpose, and whether it was a need or a want.

---
**Quote graphic:** "I measure what I manage. I improve what I measure."
