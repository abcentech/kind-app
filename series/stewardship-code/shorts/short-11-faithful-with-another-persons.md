# SHORT 11 — FAITHFUL WITH ANOTHER PERSON’S
> Companion to Episode 11 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Two students borrow cameras. One comes back clean, charged and packed. One
comes back late, scratched, missing a cover. Who gets the better camera next time?

**📖 Scripture:** "If ye have not been faithful in that which is another man’s, who shall
give you that which is your own?" — Luke 16:12

**💡 Truth:** How you treat what belongs to others reveals whether you're ready for your
own. Borrowed things are a test: ask, handle carefully, return promptly, report damage
honestly. Shared things count too — the chairs, books, computers and water that feel like
they belong to nobody actually belong to a community. Trust is what opens doors.

**🚀 Challenge:** Today, do one: return what you borrowed, repair what you damaged,
replace what you lost, or clean a shared space.
