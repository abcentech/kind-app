# SHORT 18 — GIVE MONEY AN ASSIGNMENT
> Companion to Episode 18 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** A hammer never decides what it builds. Neither does your money. Somebody gives
it direction — and if it isn’t you, it will be pressure.

**📖 Scripture:** “Make to yourselves friends of the mammon of unrighteousness.” — Luke 16:9

**💡 Truth:** Money is a tool, and tools take instructions from the hand holding them. Your
allowance, your side job, your savings can be directed to feed, educate, build, support,
create, give and solve. Income was never meant to stop at your own consumption — it should
become impact. And generosity is what breaks the illusion that money must be held at all
costs. Every time you give, your heart remembers: God is my Source. I am a steward, not an
owner.

**🚀 Challenge:** Today, use part of your money, time or skill to meet one genuine need.
Do it quietly and on purpose.
