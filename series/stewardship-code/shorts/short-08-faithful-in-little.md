# SHORT 08 — FAITHFUL IN LITTLE
> Companion to Episode 8 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** "I'll be responsible when I have real money." Careful — a leaking bucket
doesn't need more water. It needs the leak fixed.

**📖 Scripture:** "He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much."
— Luke 16:10

**💡 Truth:** Your little is not meaningless. It is the test. A bigger allowance won't
hand you discipline, wisdom or contentment — those get built *before* increase arrives.
Small amounts are the training ground where you learn to record, save, give and wait.
Don't despise the day of small things.

**🚀 Challenge:** Name three small things you already manage. For each one, write the one
habit that would prove you're faithful with it.
