# SHORT 24 — MULTIPLY, DON’T CONSUME EVERYTHING
> Companion to Episode 24 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Two teenagers get the same holiday income. One month later, one has memories.
The other has tools. Same start — different stewardship.

**📖 Scripture:** “Then came the first, saying, Lord, thy pound hath gained ten pounds.”
— Luke 19:16

**💡 Truth:** Spending isn’t the enemy. You must spend on food, transport, school,
responsibilities — and some appropriate enjoyment. The trouble starts when *everything*
gets consumed and nothing is preserved. Saving hands resources to your future self.
Investing puts resources into something that may produce future value — books, courses,
skills, tools, small projects, always with learning, patience and wise guidance. This
isn’t a trick for getting rich; it’s faithfulness with what God entrusted.

**🚀 Challenge:** For your next income, allocate it before you spend: give, save, spend,
invest, learn — setting the saving and investing portions aside first.
