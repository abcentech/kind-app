# SHORT 07 — THE OWNERSHIP COVENANT
> Companion to Episode 07 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Five days, five answers. Who owns everything? Who are you? What must you
do? Why were you given anything at all? And what happens at the end?

**📖 Scripture:** “All things come of thee, and of thine own have we given thee.”
— 1 Chronicles 29:14

**💡 Truth:** God owns everything — the world, silver and gold, your abilities, your
time, your life. He isn’t asking to become part-owner. You are the steward, and the
better question isn’t “What do I have?” but “How am I managing what belongs to God?”
Your life is an assignment, not an accident. And like a courier carrying a package that
isn’t his, you’ll be measured by whether it reached the right destination — because one
day you will give an account.

**🚀 Challenge:** Build a Personal Resource Inventory — time, money, gifts, knowledge,
devices, relationships, opportunities, influence — and write one way you’ll manage each
more faithfully.
