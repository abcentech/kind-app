# SHORT 19 — BREAK MAMMON’S CHAINS
> Companion to Episode 19 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** A prisoner’s chain can be made of gold. It still holds him. That’s the week we
just walked through — every chain money uses, and how each one breaks.

**📖 Scripture:** “Ye cannot serve God and mammon.” — Luke 16:13

**💡 Truth:** No servant serves two masters. Gold makes a poor god — it cannot forgive,
heal or satisfy. Possessions must never own your confidence. Pressure must never rename your
wants as needs. And money left without an assignment will quietly assign itself. Mammon
chains through disobedience, through identity, and through fear — being afraid of not having
enough, of losing what you hold, of being seen as poor. Wise planning is good; constant
financial fear is bondage.

**🚀 Challenge:** Practise one act of financial freedom today — give something, delay a
purchase, reject peer pressure, or thank God for what you already have.
