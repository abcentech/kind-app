# SHORT 06 — THE ACCOUNT
> Companion to Episode 06 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** No serious student waits for the examiner to walk in before starting to
learn. So why do we manage our lives like the review is never coming?

**📖 Scripture:** “Give an account of thy stewardship.” — Luke 16:2

**💡 Truth:** Every steward eventually explains what was done with what was received.
God sees how you use your time, your money, your abilities, your influence, the people
around you — nothing entrusted to you is outside His concern. And knowing a review is
coming changes how you live now: you plan better, waste less, obey faster, stay honest.
It’s also personal. Friends, background and social media influence you, but you still
answer for your own choices.

**🚀 Challenge:** Review your last seven days — time, money, internet, talents,
opportunities. Write one improvement beside each.
