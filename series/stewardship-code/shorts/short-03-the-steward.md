# SHORT 03 — THE STEWARD
> Companion to Episode 03 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** A school prefect has real authority. But the prefect does not own the
school. Guess what that makes you?

**📖 Scripture:** “There was a certain rich man, which had a steward.” — Luke 16:1

**💡 Truth:** A steward manages someone else’s resources according to the owner’s
instructions. The man in Luke 16 had access, authority and opportunity — but he never
had ownership. Access creates responsibility; it never removes accountability. And the
top quality God looks for in a manager isn’t brains or popularity or money. It’s
faithfulness — doing right consistently, even when nobody is watching.

**🚀 Challenge:** Pick one resource from yesterday’s inventory and answer three
questions: Why has God placed this in my hands? Who can it help? How can I manage it
better?
