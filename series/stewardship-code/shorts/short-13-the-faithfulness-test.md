# SHORT 13 — THE FAITHFULNESS TEST
> Companion to Episode 13 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Brushing your teeth for a whole hour once a month will not save them. This
week we learned the same thing about money — five days, one test: consistency.

**📖 Scripture:** "He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much."
— Luke 16:10

**💡 Truth:** Faithful in little. Know what you have. Plan before you spend. Protect what
belongs to others. Character before cash. That's the week — and none of it works as one
impressive action followed by weeks of carelessness. Faithfulness is right things done
repeatedly, quietly, honestly, and it leans on simple systems: records, a budget, saving,
accountability. The goal was never more money. It's greater trust, and a character ready
for whatever God places in your hands.

**🚀 Challenge:** Build your four-part Faithfulness System: what you'll record, what
you'll save, what you'll stop wasting, and who will hold you accountable.
