# SHORT 14 — TWO MASTERS
> Companion to Episode 14 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Two people cannot turn the same steering wheel in opposite directions. So who is
actually driving your money decisions?

**📖 Scripture:** “Ye cannot serve God and mammon.” — Luke 16:13

**💡 Truth:** A master has the right to command. If money is your master, it will tell you to
chase it, compromise, compare and fear. Mammon isn’t just currency — it’s the security and
identity people hand over to money. Jesus never offered a comfortable partnership. One must
sit above the other: Christ is Master, you are His steward, money is your servant.

**🚀 Challenge:** Finish this sentence honestly — “Money most often tries to control me
through __________.” Then write one biblical response to that pressure.
