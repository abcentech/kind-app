# SHORT 16 — POSSESSIONS MUST NOT POSSESS ME
> Companion to Episode 16 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** There’s a teenager who only feels confident wearing one particular brand of
shoe. That shoe isn’t being worn anymore. It’s doing the wearing.

**📖 Scripture:** “And he was sad at that saying, and went away grieved: for he had great
possessions.” — Mark 10:22

**💡 Truth:** The rich young ruler’s wealth wasn’t the whole problem. He could not release
what stood between him and obeying Jesus. Phones, shoes, clothes, schools, your online
image — all of it can be enjoyed, but none of it decides your worth. And you rarely know how
attached you are until you’re asked to share it, delay it, or live without it. A steward can
let go, because identity and security are already found in God.

**🚀 Challenge:** Pick one possession you value highly and ask: Why do I value it? How would
I feel without it? Could I release it if God required it?

---
**Quote graphic:** “I possess things. Things do not possess me.”
