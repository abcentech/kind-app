# SHORT 15 — GOLD IS NOT MY GOD
> Companion to Episode 15 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Most people quote this verse wrong. The Bible never says money is the root of
all evil.

**📖 Scripture:** “For the love of money is the root of all evil.” — 1 Timothy 6:10

**💡 Truth:** It’s the *love* of money that does the damage — craving wealth above
righteousness, measuring people by possessions, refusing contentment. Money makes a terrible
god. It cannot forgive sin, give eternal life, guarantee peace or satisfy your soul. It can
solve financial problems; it cannot carry the weight of worship. Fire in the cooker warms
the house. Fire loose in the house destroys it.

**🚀 Challenge:** List three things money can never give you. Then list three ways you can
use money responsibly this week.
