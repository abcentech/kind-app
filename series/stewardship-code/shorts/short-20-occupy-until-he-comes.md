# SHORT 20 — OCCUPY UNTIL HE COMES
> Companion to Episode 20 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Ten servants. Ten pounds. One instruction — and it wasn’t “sit still and
wait.”

**📖 Scripture:** “And he called his ten servants, and delivered them ten pounds, and
said unto them, Occupy till I come.” — Luke 19:13

**💡 Truth:** Occupy means engage productively. Jesus expects His servants to be
spiritually faithful and actually busy until He returns. Work isn’t just a way to receive
money — it serves people, builds character and can honour God. And prayer belongs with
action: pray for wisdom, plan carefully, learn diligently, work honestly. Waiting for
Christ is never an excuse for laziness.

**🚀 Challenge:** Spend at least thirty minutes today developing a productive skill.
Actual practice, not scrolling about it.
