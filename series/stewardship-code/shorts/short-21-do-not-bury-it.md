# SHORT 21 — DO NOT BURY IT
> Companion to Episode 21 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** One servant lost nothing. Not a single coin. And he still got it wrong.

**📖 Scripture:** “Lord, behold, here is thy pound, which I have kept laid up in a
napkin.” — Luke 19:20

**💡 Truth:** He handed back exactly what he received. Nothing stolen — but nothing
multiplied either. Playing safe becomes disobedience when God has told you to act. Fear
of failure, criticism or comparison keeps a gift safe and completely unproductive. Gifts
grow only through use: practise, learn, take correction, serve, repeat.

**🚀 Challenge:** Pick one gift you’ve been neglecting. Practise it for thirty minutes,
then show the result to someone you trust.
