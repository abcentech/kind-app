# SHORT 22 — DEVELOP YOUR GIFT
> Companion to Episode 22 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** The most talented player on the field isn’t always the best player by the end
of the season. Why not?

**📖 Scripture:** “Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before
kings.” — Proverbs 22:29

**💡 Truth:** Potential only means you *could* become good at something — it is not
mastery. Natural ability becomes valuable through practice, study, feedback and
correction. Diligence means careful, consistent effort: arrive prepared, practise the
basics, fix mistakes, keep learning. Excellence is usually just small acts of diligence
stacked up. Skill also creates room to serve — a developed gift is useful to people.

**🚀 Challenge:** Write a seven-day plan for one skill: what you’ll practise, how long,
what you’ll study, and who will give you honest feedback.
