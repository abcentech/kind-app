# SHORT 02 — THE OWNER
> Companion to Episode 02 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Your name is on the phone. Your face unlocks it. So why isn’t it actually
yours?

**📖 Scripture:** “The earth is the LORD’S, and the fulness thereof.” — Psalm 24:1

**💡 Truth:** Everything belongs to God — the land, the minerals, your health, your
intelligence, every opportunity that made your stuff possible. Stewardship starts the
moment you stop acting like an absolute owner. Think about how carefully you’d handle a
friend’s expensive laptop for a week. Your time, abilities and possessions deserve more
care than that, because they belong to God.

**🚀 Challenge:** Make a list titled “What God Owns but Has Placed in My Hands.” Get at
least seven items down — time, money, devices, skills, opportunities.

---
**Quote graphic:** “God is not a participant in the economy. He is the Owner.”
