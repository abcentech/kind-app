# SHORT 17 — NEEDS, WANTS AND PRESSURE
> Companion to Episode 17 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Your phone worked perfectly this morning. Then you saw your friend’s new one.
Nothing broke — so what actually changed?

**📖 Scripture:** “I have learned, in whatsoever state I am, therewith to be content.”
— Philippians 4:11

**💡 Truth:** A need keeps you healthy, safe and responsible. A want is desirable but not
essential — and a want isn’t wrong until you start calling it a need. Pressure is what blurs
the line: friends, ads, influencers, trends, fear of missing out. It turns a useful desire
into an emotional emergency. Notice Paul said, “I have learned.” Contentment isn’t giving up
on growth. It’s being grateful and peaceful while you work responsibly for better.

**🚀 Challenge:** Label every purchase you consider today: need, useful want, or
pressure-driven want. Delay every pressure-driven one for at least seven days.
