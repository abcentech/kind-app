# SHORT 26 — THE FAITHFUL STEWARD
> Companion to Episode 26 (Grand Finale) · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Thirty-one days ago we said there was a code to crack. Today — the three words
the whole month was walking toward.

**📖 Scripture:** “Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful over a
few things.” — Matthew 25:21

**💡 Truth:** The faithful steward knows three things. God owns everything — that produces
humility. Faithfulness is *active*: records, plans, honesty, developed gifts, problems
solved, resources invested. And the greatest test was never the size of your account — it
was whether Jesus Christ stayed your Master. Wealth may pass through your hands; it must
never sit on the throne of your heart.

**🚀 Challenge:** Write and sign the Stewardship Covenant. One habit you’ll begin, one waste
you’ll stop, one skill you’ll develop, one cause you’ll support.

**🔑 The Complete Code:** Ownership — God owns it; I manage it. Faithfulness — little before
much. Mastery — Christ my Master, money my servant. Multiplication — grow what He gave me.
Eternal — I will give an account.
