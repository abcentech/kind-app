# SHORT 04 — THE SOURCE
> Companion to Episode 04 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** That side job. That allowance. That gift someone dropped on you. None of
them are your Source — they’re just the pipe.

**📖 Scripture:** “It is he that giveth thee power to get wealth.” — Deuteronomy 8:18

**💡 Truth:** Money rarely falls from heaven. It arrives through work, skill,
creativity, gifts and opportunity — but behind every honest channel is the God who gives
life, health, intelligence, strength, favour and ideas. A job can end. A customer can
leave. A gift gets spent. God remains. Remembering that keeps you peaceful when a
channel changes, and humble enough never to say, “I did this all by myself.”

**🚀 Challenge:** Write down three channels God currently provides through. Beside each
one write: “God is my Source; this is only a channel.”

---
**Quote graphic:** “Never worship the channel and forget the Source.”
