# SHORT 12 — CHARACTER BEFORE CASH
> Companion to Episode 12 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** The cashier hands you too much change. Nobody noticed. Nobody will. What you
do next is not about the money.

**📖 Scripture:** "If therefore ye have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who
will commit to your trust the true riches?" — Luke 16:11

**💡 Truth:** Money tests character. It rarely creates greed, fear or dishonesty — it
exposes what was already there, along with generosity and self-control. So never trade
your character for extra change, exam malpractice, online fraud or a secret commission;
money gained through corruption costs more than it pays. Jesus pointed to "true riches" —
wisdom, integrity, trust — and those are handed only to the trustworthy.

**🚀 Challenge:** Review your recent money moves and correct one dishonest thing —
change, borrowing, a sale, a school payment, shared money.
