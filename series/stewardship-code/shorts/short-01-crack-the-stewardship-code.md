# SHORT 01 — CRACK THE STEWARDSHIP CODE
> Companion to Episode 01 (Series Launch) · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** Everybody serves a master. The scary part? Most people never find out who
theirs is until money asks them to choose.

**📖 Scripture:** “No servant can serve two masters… Ye cannot serve God and mammon.”
— Luke 16:13

**💡 Truth:** Jesus never said having money is wrong — He said *serving* it is. A master is
whatever controls your choices and commands your loyalty. So the first stewardship question
isn’t “How much do I have?” It’s “Who rules my heart?” Money is like a powerful car with no
driver: brilliant when it’s directed, destructive when it takes the wheel. Keep it in your
hand and off the throne.

**🚀 Challenge:** Answer three questions honestly: What do I believe money can do for me?
What financial pressure controls my emotions? How can I make money serve God’s purpose?

**📅 This month:** 26 episodes. Four Codes — Ownership, Faithfulness, Mastery,
Multiplication. Let’s crack it.
