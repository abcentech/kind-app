# SHORT 25 — INVEST FOR ETERNITY
> Companion to Episode 25 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** All week we’ve been multiplying. Now the question that decides what it was
all for: where does it go when you can’t take it with you?

**📖 Scripture:** “Make to yourselves friends of the mammon of unrighteousness; that,
when ye fail, they may receive you into everlasting habitations.” — Luke 16:9

**💡 Truth:** Occupy till He comes. Don’t bury the gift. Develop it with diligence. Use it
to solve a real problem. Multiply instead of consuming everything. But here is where the
week lands: money is temporary — it gets spent, lost, outdated, left behind. People are
not. Invest in people through education, mentoring, encouragement, training, the gospel,
and the greatest return won’t be measured in money at all. The highest purpose of
multiplication was never personal accumulation. It’s eternal impact.

**🚀 Challenge:** Write your Personal Multiplication Plan: one gift to develop, one
problem to solve, one amount to save, one investment to study, one person to help, one
Kingdom project to support.

**Quote graphic:** “Temporary resources, eternal purposes. Send your treasure on ahead.”
