# SHORT 10 — PLAN BEFORE YOU SPEND
> Companion to Episode 10 · *The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds* · 30–60s

**Opening:** "Welcome to **The Stewardship Code in 60 Seconds**."

**🎯 Hook:** No football team runs onto the pitch with everyone chasing the ball wherever
they feel like. They use a formation. So does your money.

**📖 Scripture:** "Which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and
counteth the cost?" — Luke 14:28

**💡 Truth:** A budget is a plan that gives money an assignment before emotions spend it.
Jesus praised the builder who counted the cost first — planning is not a lack of faith.
Without a plan, the loudest desire takes everything. With one, giving, saving, spending,
investing and learning each keep their place. A budget isn't punishment; it's permission
to spend without regret.

**🚀 Challenge:** Before your next allowance or side-job pay arrives, assign every part of
it: give, save, spend, invest, learn.
