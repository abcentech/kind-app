## THE TWO LINES THAT FRAME EVERY EPISODE

**OPEN — say this at the top of all 26 episodes, word for word:**
> *"Your SuperPower is not what you wish you could do. Your SuperPower is the capacity you have taken time to develop."*

**CLOSE — say this at the end of all 26 episodes, word for word:**
> *"Don't wait for your Goliath to start developing your sling."*

These two lines are the identity of the series. Do not paraphrase them, do not shorten them, do not skip them when an episode runs long. By 30 September every child should be able to finish both sentences before the presenter does.

---

## A GUARDRAIL ON THE WORD "SUPERPOWER"

The framing is strong and the children will love it — but a superpower story can quietly become a story about self. Keep this rule and it never will:

**The SuperPower is developed by the child; the victory is delivered by God.**

David developed a sling. God delivered the giant. Both are true, and the order matters: *"the LORD saveth not with sword and spear: for the battle is the LORD's"* (1 Samuel 17:47). Every episode develops something in the child, and every episode ends by handing the outcome to Christ. That is what the **CHRIST AT THE CENTRE** segment is for, and it is non-negotiable in all 26 episodes.

Say it plainly to the children in Week 1 and again in Week 5: *"Your SuperPower is not so you can be amazing. Your SuperPower is so you can be useful to God."*

### The daily episode shape (Mon–Sat · released 6:00pm)

**Cold open first. Branding second. Teaching third.** Nothing — no logo, no greeting, no "welcome back to another episode" — comes before the hook. The first frame of every video is the hook.

```
0:00 – 0:15   COLD OPEN HOOK    No branding, no greeting. Straight in.
0:15 – 0:26   ★ THE BUMPER      11s animated series bumper (Remotion)

0:46 – 1:16   THE RHYME         Full cumulative chant to date

9:45 – 10:00  THE CLOSE LINE    "Don't wait for your Goliath to start developing your sling."
```

**Why the hook comes before the branding.** On YouTube and on WhatsApp status you have about three seconds before a viewer decides. A logo animation spends those three seconds on something the viewer already knows. The hook spends them on a question the viewer wants answered — and then the bumper lands on a viewer who has already decided to stay.

**The bumper doubles as the title card.** It is data-driven: pass it the day number and it renders the week, the SuperPower, the day, and the episode title on the end frame. You never design a title card by hand. Full build spec in the companion document, *Trailer & Bumper — Production Spec.*

### The month trailer
A 75-second trailer releases **Friday 28 August at 6:00pm** — four days before Day 1 — and again as a pinned post on 1 September. It is not shown inside episodes; the 11-second bumper is the in-episode version, cut from the same assets.

### The devotional — 8:00pm daily, including Sundays
Two hours after the video. The video **develops**; the devotional **turns it inward**. One scripture, one question, under four minutes.

### Sunday discussion — Sep 6, 13, 20, 27
No new teaching. Chant the rhyme, speak the week's declaration, take testimonies from the Capacity Challenges, answer questions, and mark the journey map.

# WEEK 1 — THE CAVE
## Developing in Private
### SuperPower: **COMPETENCE** · September 1–5 · Rhyme: Stanza 1

> **David became dangerous before anybody knew his name.**

**Weekly Declaration:** *"I will develop privately until I can deliver publicly."*

**★ A gift for this week's title:** David's literal cave comes later — Adullam, in 1 Samuel 22:1–2, where four hundred men *"in distress… in debt… discontented"* gathered to him and eventually became his mighty men. Mention it on Saturday: *"The cave where David hid became the cave where an army was born. Nothing God does in your hidden place is wasted."* It gives the week's name real biblical ground and it lands beautifully.

### DAY 1 · Tuesday 1 September
## "Hidden Before Heard"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 16:11
**Toni's lesson:** *David was not even invited when Samuel came. Never despise hidden seasons.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:12):** A full table, everyone eating, one empty chair. Camera settles on the chair. Presenter, quiet: *"Somebody in this family is missing — and the prophet of God refuses to start until he arrives."* Beat. Straight to lens: *"Have you ever been the empty chair?"*

**The Three**
1. **Being left out is not being left behind.** Seven sons were presented to a prophet and nobody sent for David. God stopped the entire ceremony until the forgotten one arrived.
2. **Hidden is not the same as forgotten.** He was in a field, not in disgrace. God knew the address.
3. **The anointing came before the crown.** Where you are is not who you are.

**OT Mirror:** Joseph — youngest, sold, forgotten in a pit and a prison, while God wrote "Prime Minister" over him (Genesis 37; 41:41).
**NT Mirror:** *"Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers"* (1 Timothy 4:12).

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** Jesus grew up in Nazareth — the town people asked, *"Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?"* (John 1:46); *"he is despised and rejected of men"* (Isaiah 53:3). If they overlooked the Saviour of the world, being overlooked cannot disqualify you.

**Capacity Challenge:** Write your name on a card. Underneath, write one thing God has said about you that people cannot see yet. Keep it in your school bag all September.

**8pm Devotional:** *1 Samuel 16:7.* **What is in your heart today that only God can see — and are you glad He can see it?**

**Shoot note:** Establish the "cave" visual — a quiet, enclosed, private space. Reuse it every day this week so the children *see* where they are.

### DAY 2 · Wednesday 2 September
## "Faithful With Sheep"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:15
**Toni's lesson:** *Before David managed a nation, he managed sheep. Your current assignment is training for your next one.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:10):** Presenter holds a mop in one hand and a microphone in the other. Lifts each in turn. *"One of these is the reason you will ever get the other."* Beat. *"Guess which one comes first."*

**The Three**
1. **He went back.** *"But David went and returned from Saul to feed his father's sheep at Bethlehem."* He had been in a palace and he still went back to the sheep. Competence does not get offended by small work.
2. **"Those few sheep" was a real assignment.** Eliab later said it as an insult (17:28). God called it training.
3. **Small things are the entrance exam.** *"He chose David also his servant, and took him from the sheepfolds"* (Psalm 78:70). Not around them. From them.

**OT Mirror:** Ruth gleaning in a corner of a field she did not own — and that ordinary field placed her in the family line of Christ (Ruth 2:2–3; Matthew 1:5).
**NT Mirror:** *"He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much"* (Luke 16:10).

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** Jesus spent about thirty years in a carpenter's shop and about three in public ministry — ten times more hidden faithfulness than public ministry. And He is *"the good shepherd"* who *"giveth his life for the sheep"* (John 10:11). He is not asking you to do anything He has not already done.

**Capacity Challenge:** Pick the small duty at home you usually avoid. Do it today, unasked and unannounced.

**8pm Devotional:** *Luke 16:10.* **Name the "few sheep" God has given you this season. Have you been feeding them?**

### DAY 3 · Thursday 3 September
## "The Lion Lesson"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:34
**Toni's lesson:** *David learned courage fighting what nobody celebrated. Small battles build big capacity.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:12):** Total black. A lion's roar. A small voice in the dark: *"It's just me and it out here."* Cut to presenter: *"Nobody clapped. Nobody filmed it. Nobody even knew."* Beat. *"And it is the reason Goliath died."*

**The Three**
1. **The lion came for what he was carrying.** It took a lamb *out of the flock.* Attacks often target your assignment, not just you.
2. **He went after it himself.** No report, no negotiation, no waiting for reinforcements. Competence deals with what is in front of it.
3. **Nobody clapped.** There was no crowd, no camera, no prize. That is exactly why it counted.

**OT Mirror:** Samson tore a lion in a vineyard with nobody watching (Judges 14:5–6) — a private victory before a public deliverance.
**NT Mirror:** *"Your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about... whom resist stedfast in the faith"* (1 Peter 5:8–9).

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** There is a Lion who fights *for* us — *"the Lion of the tribe of Juda hath prevailed"* (Revelation 5:5). David fought one; we belong to one. Every private battle you win, you win on His strength.

**Capacity Challenge:** Name one "lion" — a habit, a fear, a temptation that keeps stealing from you. Tell God about it tonight, by name.

**8pm Devotional:** *1 Peter 5:8–9.* **What has been taken from you that God wants you to go back and recover?**

**Shoot note:** Silhouette and sound. Suggest the danger; do not stage it.

### DAY 4 · Friday 4 September
## "The Bear Lesson"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:35–36
**Toni's lesson:** *One victory became preparation for another. Every challenge can become training.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:09):** Presenter counts slowly on three fingers. *"Lion."* *"Bear."* *"Giant."* Beat, straight to lens: *"God never sends the giant first."*

**The Three**
1. **God repeats a lesson until the lesson is in you.** The lion taught him. The bear proved he had learned.
2. **He recovered what was taken** — *"I went out after him, and smote him, and delivered it out of his mouth."* God builds recoverers, not just survivors.
3. **The second one was bigger and he was not afraid.** That is the definition of growing capacity: the next thing is harder and it no longer frightens you.

**OT Mirror:** Moses given two signs at the burning bush — the rod and the hand — so the first would be believed (Exodus 4:1–9).
**NT Mirror:** *"Who delivered us from so great a death, and doth deliver: in whom we trust that he will yet deliver us"* (2 Corinthians 1:10) — past, present, future, one God.

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** Jesus was tested three times in the wilderness before He preached one sermon (Matthew 4:1–11), and returned *"in the power of the Spirit"* (Luke 4:14). Private test, then public power. Always that order.

**Capacity Challenge:** Start your **lion-and-bear page** — write down one hard thing God has already brought you through. Add to it all month.

**8pm Devotional:** *1 Samuel 17:37.* **What has God already delivered you from that you have stopped thanking Him for?**

### DAY 5 · Saturday 5 September — **WEEK 1 CLOSES**
## "Practise Your Sling"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:40
**Toni's lesson:** *David's weapon worked because he had used it before. Develop your gift until it becomes dependable.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:14):** Presenter throws a stone at a target and misses badly. Throws again — misses. Again — misses. Turns to camera, breathless: *"Now imagine doing this ten thousand times before anybody is watching."* Beat. *"That is the entire difference between David and me."*

**The Three**
1. **A sling is only a weapon in a practised hand.** In an untrained hand it is a piece of leather. The tool is not the SuperPower — the *developed* tool is.
2. **Dependable beats impressive.** David's sling was not the most powerful weapon in that valley. It was the most reliable one *he* had.
3. **You cannot practise during the fight.** Whatever you have not developed before the giant will not be available in front of the giant.

**OT Mirror:** *"What is that in thine hand?"* — a shepherd's rod, used daily for forty years, became the rod of God (Exodus 4:2).
**NT Mirror:** *"Strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised"* (Hebrews 5:14). Use is what makes it strong.

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** *"Though he were a Son, yet learned he obedience by the things which he suffered"* (Hebrews 5:8) — so we have a High Priest *"touched with the feeling of our infirmities"* (Hebrews 4:15). Jesus was proved in real conditions so that He can help you in yours.

**★ THE CAVE, LITERALLY:** Read **1 Samuel 22:1–2** — years later David hid in the cave of Adullam, and four hundred distressed, indebted, discontented men came to him there. That cave produced his mighty men. Tell them: *"The cave where David hid became the cave where an army was born. Nothing God does in your hidden place is ever wasted."*

**Capacity Challenge:** Name **one gift** God has given you. Commit to practising it for fifteen minutes a day, every day, to the end of September.

**8pm Devotional:** *Hebrews 5:14.* **Which of your gifts have you never actually developed?**

**Weekly Declaration:** *"I know who I am, whose I am, and what God has put inside me."*

### DAY 6 · Monday 7 September
## "Who Are You?"
**Text:** Psalm 139:13–16; Jeremiah 1:5
**Toni's lesson:** *Your capacity begins with identity.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:13):** Presenter hands a child a blank name tag and a marker. *"Write who you are. Not your name — who you ARE."* The child hesitates, pen hovering. Hold the silence. Presenter to camera: *"That pause is what this whole week is about."*

**The Three**
1. **You were made on purpose, with purpose.** *"I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made"* (Psalm 139:14). Nothing about you is an accident.
2. **God knew you before anybody named you.** *"Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee"* (Jeremiah 1:5). Your identity is older than your birthday.
3. **Capacity is discovered, not invented.** You are not trying to become somebody. You are learning what God already put in you.

**OT Mirror:** Gideon was hiding in a winepress when the angel called him *"thou mighty man of valour"* (Judges 6:12). God named him by his future, not his hiding place.
**NT Mirror:** *"We are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them"* (Ephesians 2:10).

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** Jesus knew exactly who He was — *"I know whence I came, and whither I go"* (John 8:14) — and He is the one who gives us our identity: *"as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God"* (John 1:12). You do not find yourself. You find Him, and He tells you who you are.

**Capacity Challenge:** Write three true sentences that begin *"I am…"* — using only what God's word says about you.

**8pm Devotional:** *Psalm 139:14.* **Do you believe you were made on purpose? What would change today if you did?**

### DAY 7 · Tuesday 8 September
## "Whose Are You?"
**Text:** Psalm 100:3
**Toni's lesson:** *You belong to God before you belong to any crowd.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:11):** Presenter holds up a lost item found on the floor. *"No name on it. No owner. So anybody can pick it up — and anybody can throw it away."* Beat. *"Do you know who owns you?"*

**The Three**
1. **He made us, not we ourselves.** *"Know ye that the LORD he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture."*
2. **Belonging settles you.** A child who knows whose they are stops auditioning for every crowd.
3. **Ownership means covering.** If you are His sheep, He is your shepherd — and shepherds fight lions.

**OT Mirror:** *"Fear not: for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine"* (Isaiah 43:1).
**NT Mirror:** *"Ye are not your own... for ye are bought with a price"* (1 Corinthians 6:19–20).

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** *"My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me... neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand"* (John 10:27–28). The price of your belonging was His blood. That is how secure your identity is.

**Capacity Challenge:** Notice one moment today when you were tempted to change who you are to fit in. Choose differently.

**8pm Devotional:** *Psalm 100:3.* **Whose approval have you been working hardest for this year?**

### DAY 8 · Wednesday 9 September
## "Uncircumcised Philistine"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:26
**Toni's lesson:** *David interpreted Goliath through covenant.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:12):** Two cards slap onto a table. **"NINE FEET TALL."** **"NO COVENANT."** Presenter: *"Both of these are true about Goliath. One of them is about to get him killed."* Beat. *"Which one?"*

**The Three**
1. **David measured Goliath against God, not himself.** Against David, Goliath was huge. Against *"the armies of the living God,"* he was nothing.
2. **Covenant is the real measurement.** His confidence was not in his size but in his side.
3. **Naming things correctly disarms them.** Everyone else said "the champion." David said "this uncircumcised Philistine" — a man with no covenant and therefore no covering.

**OT Mirror:** Jonathan, two men against a garrison: *"there is no restraint to the LORD to save by many or by few"* (1 Samuel 14:6).
**NT Mirror:** *"Greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world"* (1 John 4:4).

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** We are in a better covenant — *"a surety of a better testament"* (Hebrews 7:22) — sealed by Christ's own blood. At the cross He *"spoiled principalities and powers... triumphing over them in it"* (Colossians 2:15). Your giants are already-defeated enemies still making noise.

**Capacity Challenge:** Write your giant's name. Beside it write: *"compared to the living God, you are ______."* Fill it in truthfully.

**8pm Devotional:** *1 John 4:4.* **Are you measuring your problem against yourself, or against God?**

### DAY 9 · Thursday 10 September
## "Don't Wear Saul's Armour"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:38–39
**Toni's lesson:** *Don't lose yourself trying to become somebody else.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:15):** A small child buried in an adult's coat, helmet and boots tries to walk three steps and topples over. Hold the shot — let it be funny. Presenter turns to camera, completely deadpan: *"Somebody is dressing you like this right now."* Beat. *"And you are saying thank you."*

**The Three**
1. **Saul dressed David like Saul.** *"And Saul armed David with his armour."* People will always try to reproduce themselves in you.
2. **What protects one person can paralyse another.** The armour was not bad. It was not *his.*
3. **"I cannot go with these" is a complete sentence.** Said respectfully, to a king — and then he took it off. Confidence knows how to decline.

**OT Mirror:** Aaron's garments were made *"for glory and for beauty"* and fitted to specific men (Exodus 28:2–4). God has never done one-size-fits-all.
**NT Mirror:** *"There are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit... the body is not one member, but many"* (1 Corinthians 12:4, 14). Comparison is a category error.

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** Jesus refused every costume offered Him — He would not be made king by force (John 6:15), and would not come down from the cross to prove Himself (Matthew 27:40–42). He stayed inside His own assignment, and that is precisely how we were saved.

**Capacity Challenge:** Name one thing you are only doing because somebody else does it. Take it off this week.

**8pm Devotional:** *1 Corinthians 12:14.* **Whose armour have you been wearing?**

### DAY 10 · Friday 11 September
## "Remember Your Testimony"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:37
**Toni's lesson:** *"The LORD that delivered me…" Confidence grows when you remember what God has already done.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:11):** Presenter scrolls a phone gallery fast — hundreds of photos blurring past. Stops dead. Looks up. *"You keep a record of everything."* Beat. *"Where do you keep the record of what God has done?"*

**The Three**
1. **Testimony is the answer to intimidation.** *"The LORD that delivered me out of the paw of the lion, and out of the paw of the bear, he will deliver me out of the hand of this Philistine."*
2. **Past deliverance is a promise, not a memory.** He used a finished act of God as evidence for an unfinished one.
3. **Keep receipts.** If you cannot recall what God has done, you will over-fear what is in front of you.

**OT Mirror:** Samuel set up a stone and called it Ebenezer — *"Hitherto hath the LORD helped us"* (1 Samuel 7:12).
**NT Mirror:** *"They overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony"* (Revelation 12:11).

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** The greatest testimony ever given is an empty tomb — *"He is not here: for he is risen"* (Matthew 28:6). David testified to a lion and a bear. We testify to a resurrection. If death could not hold Him, your giant cannot hold you.

**★ Teacher's note:** This is where Week 1 pays for Week 2. Put the cave footage from Days 3–4 back on screen while he speaks. Make the children *feel* that the private season became the public argument.

**Capacity Challenge:** Open your lion-and-bear page from Day 4. Read it out loud to one person today.

**8pm Devotional:** *Revelation 12:11.* **What is your testimony? Have you told anyone?**

### DAY 11 · Saturday 12 September — **WEEK 2 CLOSES**
## "I Come in His Name"
**Text:** 1 Samuel 17:45
**Toni's lesson:** *David's greatest confidence wasn't his sling; it was God's Name.*

**COLD OPEN HOOK (0:00–0:10):** Presenter holds a sling in one hand and nothing in the other. Raises the sling — *"This was his weapon."* Raises the empty hand — *"This is where his confidence was."*

**The Three**
1. **Goliath came with hardware; David came with a Name.** *"Thou comest to me with a sword, and with a spear, and with a shield: but I come to thee in the name of the LORD of hosts, the God of the armies of Israel, whom thou hast defied."*
2. **He developed a sling and depended on a Name.** Both. Never one without the other — that is the whole balance of this series.
3. **The Name settles the outcome before the fight.** *"This day will the LORD deliver thee into mine hand."*

**OT Mirror:** *"Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the LORD our God"* (Psalm 20:7) — written by this same David, years later. He never changed weapons.
**NT Mirror:** *"In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth rise up and walk"* (Acts 3:6). No silver, no gold — the Name.

**CHRIST AT THE CENTRE:** David borrowed that Name by faith. We know it by revelation: *"God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name"* (Philippians 2:9–11). **Jesus.** That is the Name your SuperPower runs on.

**Capacity Challenge:** Before today's hardest moment, say out loud: *"I come in the name of the Lord."*

**8pm Devotional:** *Philippians 2:9–11.* **What are you trusting in that is not the Name of the Lord?**

# WEEK 3 — THE CAUSE
## Find Something Bigger Than Yourself
### SuperPower: **CONVICTION** · September 14–19 · Rhyme: Stanzas 1 + 2 + 3

> **Anchor — 1 Samuel 17:29:** *"Is there not a cause?"*

**Weekly Declaration:** *"My life will solve a problem bigger than myself."*

**★ Teacher's note for the week:** This is the week that turns a nice series into a life direction. Do not rush Friday and Saturday — those two episodes are where children start naming what they were born to do. # APPENDIX C — RULES OF THE SERIES (for every teacher on camera)

1. **Hook first. Always.** No logo, no greeting, no "welcome back" before the cold open. The first frame of the video is the hook. The bumper comes after it.
2. **The open line and the close line, word for word, in all 26 episodes.** They are the identity of the series.
3. **Three points. Never four.** A fourth point is next week's episode.
4. **Two mirrors, every day.** One Old Testament, one New Testament. No exceptions.
5. **Christ at the centre, not David.** David is the illustration; Jesus is the point. If an episode could be preached by someone who does not know Christ, rewrite it.
6. **The SuperPower is developed by the child; the victory is delivered by God.** Never let the framing drift into "you are amazing." It is always "you are useful, and He is able."
7. **Never end on effort. End on grace.**
8. **Speak to the one child in front of you.** Second person singular. "You."
9. **One action, not five.** The Capacity Challenge must be doable before tomorrow's episode.
10. **Chant the full rhyme every day.** It is the memory device that carries this month into their whole year.
11. **Say the weekly declaration together, on camera, every episode.**

# APPENDIX D — THE CHRIST THREAD
### If a teacher reads only one page, make it this one

David is not the hero of 1 Samuel 17. He is the shadow — *"a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ"* (Colossians 2:17).

| David | Fulfilled in Christ |
|---|---|
| The overlooked eighth son | The despised Nazarene — Isaiah 53:3; John 1:46 |
| Developed in a hidden field | Thirty hidden years in Nazareth — Luke 2:51–52 |
| Sent by his father to his brothers | *"God sent forth his Son"* — Galatians 4:4 |
| Rejected by his own brothers | *"Neither did his brethren believe in him"* — John 7:5 |
| Refused borrowed armour | Refused to come down from the cross — Matthew 27:40–42 |
| Ran toward the enemy alone | *"He stedfastly set his face to go to Jerusalem"* — Luke 9:51 |
| One representative fought for all | *"By one man's obedience shall many be made righteous"* — Romans 5:19 |
| Killed the enemy with his own sword | Destroyed death by death — Hebrews 2:14–15 |
| An army arose and pursued | *"We are more than conquerors"* — Romans 8:37 |
| Victory shared with those who did nothing | Grace through faith — Ephesians 2:8–9 |
| One giant-killer produced many | *"Greater works than these shall he do"* — John 14:12 |

The children are not being asked to become David. They are being told they stand behind a greater David who has already won — **and that is exactly why they can walk into 2026/2027 with capacity.**
